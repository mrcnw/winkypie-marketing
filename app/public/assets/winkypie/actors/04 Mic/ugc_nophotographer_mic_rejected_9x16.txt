Rejected on the owner's note: the microphone has to go and the hands have to move. A man holding
a stick mic reads as a news anchor, and the reference format's whole appeal is a person talking
like a person. Cropping the prop below the frame edge does not fix the register; removing it
does.

Two things this take proved, which is why it is here and not deleted:

1. The green screen on portrait_green.png does not fill the frame. The backdrop stops at about
   0.55 of the frame height and a bright grey floor sits below it - sampled (212,207,202) where
   green was expected. A cutout crop had to stop at y 445 of 864 to stay on green. Invisible in
   the portrait, which is framed tighter than what Seedance generates around it.
2. The model pads a short script by repeating a sentence. Thirty-two words filled about 13.4 s
   of the 15 s and it said "Same build." twice, at 0:08 and again at 0:09, to reach the ceiling.
   Confirmed at word level, not a transcription artefact.

Its key needed chromakey=0x0A5A2C:0.10:0.02 - a different value from every later clip. Five
takes across three runs, five different greens: sample the corners and sweep, every time.

Raw UGC take, 15.1 s, 496x864, native audio. Seedance 2.0 Mini, 480p, 9:16,
generate_audio true, 7.5 credits. No key applied and no overlays - this is exactly what the
platform delivered, kept so the finished cut can be rebuilt without paying for the clip again.

Key that works on these clips: chromakey=0x1A9A46:0.14:0.02 then despill=type=green. One pass,
never two - a chained second pass on a darker green removes the actor entirely.
