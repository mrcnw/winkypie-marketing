End card for the cutout-wall video set, built 2026-09-25. 1080x1920, the last 3.8 s of every
WP_P2_PORTFOLIO_* cut in creatives/ugc/.

Replaces the plain "TRY IT YOURSELF" card that shipped with the first two builds, which was set
in Arial Black on black and carried no brand at all. This one is on the kit: the mark, the
wordmark in Fraunces, and a full-round gradient pill CTA reading "Try it now" - the pattern
BRAND.md section on UI components specifies. One gradient, linear-gradient(135deg, #F59E0B,
#EC4899), used on the wordmark and the pill and nowhere else.

Fraunces and Inter are the shipped brand faces and both are SIL OFL 1.1. They are not installed
on this machine; the build fetches them from the Google Fonts repository into the scratchpad.
Drawn in PIL, not HTML - this Mac's ffmpeg has no drawtext filter, and PIL renders the gradient
through a text mask rather than through background-clip.

The word "free" is deliberately absent, so nothing on the card is purchase-adjacent and
PRODUCT.md 11.3 has nothing to attach to. The AI line is required and stays.
