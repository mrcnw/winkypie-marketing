## The loop in one paragraph
Mine the user's own words. Turn one into an angle, the angle into a brief, the brief into a creative. Pass QA and name it properly, or it never ships. Write the hypothesis **before** you launch, then leave it alone through the learning phase. At the threshold, read the funnel top-down and fix only the worst step. Scale, kill, or iterate — and write the lesson either way. The lesson feeds next week's research.
