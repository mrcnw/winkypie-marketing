# Weekly Rhythm

Fixed slots. The loop only works if research and shipping happen whether or not anyone feels like it.

| Day | Slot | Task | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | 30 min | Update [[Tracker]]. Kill / scale / hold on every live ad. | decisions logged |
| **Tue** | 30 min | [[Swipe Method]] — Ad Library, Reddit, reviews | 5 raw quotes → 3 hooks |
| **Wed** | 2 h | Brief + produce the batch → [[Production Pipeline]] | 3 creatives through QA |
| **Thu** | 1 h | Launch tests → [[Creative Testing Protocol]]. Hypothesis written first. | rows in [[Experiment Log]] |
| **Fri** | 45 min | [[Weekly Report]]. Close experiments. Empty `00 Inbox`. | report + lessons |

## Monthly
- Refresh [[Competitor Landscape]] — one new profile or teardown
- Review [[Benchmarks]] against our own trailing data
- [[ASO]] check: Store CVR trend, screenshot test

## Batch rule
One batch per week: **3 new hooks × 1 winning body**, or **1 new body × 3 proven hooks**. Never both at once.
