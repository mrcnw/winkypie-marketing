# Swipe Method

How we source angles instead of inventing them.

## Sources
1. **Meta Ad Library** - competitors' long-running ads = ads that work. -> [[Competitor Landscape]]
2. **Reddit mining** - r/Tinder, r/hingeapp, r/OnlineDating, r/dating_advice. Search for photo complaints. Copy their exact words.
3. **App Store reviews** - ours and competitors'. 3-star reviews are the goldmine.
4. **TikTok** - dating-advice creators talking about profile photos.

## Rule
The hook is the user's sentence, not ours. Paste the raw quote into [[Hooks & Angles]] before you polish it.

## Cadence
30 minutes, every Tuesday. Output: 5 raw quotes -> 3 hooks -> 1 brief.
