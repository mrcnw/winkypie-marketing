# Hooks & Angles

Bank of first-3-second openers. Tag each with the angle from [[Messaging Matrix]]. Move winners to the top, strike losers.

Voice check: short sentences, hard stops, no exclamation marks, addressed to a man.

## Pain-first
- "Your best photo is a group shot. That's the problem."
- "Three years of selfies. Not one usable photo."
- "You're not getting skipped for your personality."

## Research-first (A1)
- "She decided in 100 ms. Before she even read your name."
- "First photo. First chance."

## Demo-first (A3, A5)
- "One selfie. Watch."
- "This took 30 seconds."
- "Same face. Different photo."

## Objection-first (A4)
- "It's not a filter. That's the point."
- "Still your face. Still your build."

## Contrarian
- "Stop taking more selfies."
- "You don't need a photographer. You need one good photo."
