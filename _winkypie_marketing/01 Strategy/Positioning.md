# Positioning

## One-liner
WinkyPie turns one selfie into pro-grade dating-profile photos in ~30 seconds. A real photo, not a filter.

## Category
iOS AI photo studio. Not a headshot tool, not a filter app, not a dating app.

## The job to be done
**Win the first 100 ms.** She decides before she reads your name. The product exists to make the first photo in the stack the one that stops the scroll.

## What we actually sell
Not "AI photos". We sell **being picked**. The photo is the mechanism.

| Layer | Line |
|---|---|
| Mechanism | One selfie. Pick a pose. AI renders you in it. |
| Benefit | A first photo that stops the scroll. |
| Identity | Be the right swipe. |

## Differentiators
- **One selfie** - not 20 uploads, not a 2-hour training job.
- **~30 seconds** - competitors make you wait hours.
- **Your face, your build** - proportions preserved, not a generic model.
- **Curated pose collection** - or upload any reference you like.

## Proof strategy
Research, not testimonials. Credibility comes from citable third-party numbers (Hinge, Match, image-engagement studies). Every stat links to `/methodology`.
See [[Claims & Proof]] before using any number in an ad.

## Anti-positioning
We are **not**: a matchmaking guarantee, a catfishing tool, a beauty filter, a professional-headshot/LinkedIn product.
