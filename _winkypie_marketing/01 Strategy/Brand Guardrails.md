# Brand Guardrails

**Read before writing any ad, caption, or landing section.**

## Voice
Short sentences. Hard stops. Clipped, confident, zero filler. Fragments encouraged. Periods do the work commas would do elsewhere. Never oversell - the research persuades.

Two-beat rhythm is the house style: **a statement, then the payoff.**
> "One selfie. ~30 seconds. Photos ready for your profile."

| It is | It isn't |
|---|---|
| Direct, plain-spoken, a little blunt | Hypey or bro-y. No "crush it", no "10x your matches", no exclamation marks |
| Honest about being AI - "Stylized AI representations, not a filter" | Promising outcomes. Never "guaranteed matches" |
| Second person, addressed to a man - "Your face. Your build." | Gender-neutral or corporate-plural. No "our users" |

## Non-negotiable
1. **Mobile is canonical.** Copy flows app -> web -> ads. Never the reverse. Drift = brand drift.
2. **Men only.** No female imagery in poses, gallery, hero, or before/after.
3. **iOS-first.** One App Store badge above the fold as primary conversion path. No "coming soon to Android".
4. **One gradient.** `135deg, #f59e0b -> #ec4899`. Never a second brand gradient.

## Never ship
- **Fake social proof.** No invented user counts, fabricated testimonials, fake star ratings. FTC and EU UCPD both enforce this. "Fresh launch - be among the first" is honest; "12,000 happy users" is a lawsuit.
- **Dollar pricing on the web.** Pricing changes; the App Store listing is authoritative. Say "free trial" plus the cancellation path - nothing more.
- **Unsourced statistics.** A new percentage requires a new citable entry in `/methodology`. -> [[Claims & Proof]]
- **Off-brand copy.** Generic marketing lines with no counterpart in the mobile app.

## Ad-specific additions
- No before/after that implies a different person. The face is preserved - show that.
- No copy implying we know the viewer's dating status ("Still single?"). Meta Personal Attributes policy. -> [[Meta Ad Policy]]
