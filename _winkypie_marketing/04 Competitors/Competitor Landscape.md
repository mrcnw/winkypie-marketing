## How to research
1. **App Store**: search "AI dating photos", "AI headshot", "profile picture AI". Record the top 10.
2. **Meta Ad Library**: [facebook.com/ads/library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/) - filter by country, search the brand. **Ads running 30+ days are proven winners.** Screenshot into `Swipes/`.
3. **App Store reviews**: read the 1- and 3-star reviews. Their complaints are our angles.
4. **Their landing page**: run a [[Competitor Teardown]].

## Watchlist cadence
Monthly. One profile note per competitor in `Profiles/`, created from `_Templates/Competitor Profile`.

## What we're looking for
- Which **angle** they lead with (speed? quality? price? dating-specific?)
- Whether they show **before/after** and how they handle the AI-disclosure problem
- Their **offer** (free trial? credits? one-off?)
- Ad **longevity** = what's working
- Gaps we can own: one selfie, ~30s, dating-specific, men-only, iOS-native
