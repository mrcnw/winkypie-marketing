# Funnel & North Star

## North star
**Paid subscribers acquired per week, at CAC below payback threshold.**
Not installs. Installs are a vanity number for a subscription app.

## Funnel
| Step | Metric | Owner | Lever |
|---|---|---|---|
| See ad | Impressions, CPM | media buying | budget, audience |
| Stop | Hook rate | creative | [[Hooks & Angles]] |
| Click | CTR | creative | [[Copy Bank]] |
| Store page | Store CVR | ASO | [[ASO]] |
| Install | CPI | all above | |
| First shot | Activation % | product | onboarding |
| Trial | Install->Trial | product | paywall placement |
| Paid | Trial->Paid | product/pricing | [[Lifecycle & Push]] |
| Retained | D30, churn | product | new poses |

## Weakest-link rule
Only work on the step with the worst conversion relative to its benchmark. Improving a step that isn't the bottleneck moves nothing.
