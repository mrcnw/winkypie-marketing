# ASO — App Store Optimization

The highest-leverage channel for an iOS app, and the cheapest. Every paid click lands on this page — a Store CVR gain multiplies **all** ad spend.

## Keyword candidates
`ai photos` · `dating photos` · `profile picture` · `ai headshot` · `selfie ai` · `dating profile` · `photo ai` · `hinge photos` · `tinder photos`
> Check trademark rules before using competitor/dating-app brand names in metadata.

## Screenshots — the real conversion driver
- [ ] First 2 screenshots carry the whole pitch (that's all most people see)
- [ ] Caption text large enough to read in the search grid
- [ ] Screenshot 1 = "One selfie. Every angle."
- [ ] Men only
- [ ] App Preview video, 15-30s, works silent
