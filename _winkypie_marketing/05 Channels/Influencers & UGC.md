# Influencers & UGC Creators

Our creative bottleneck is footage of real men. UGC creators solve it faster than in-house production.

## Creator brief essentials
- Male creators only
- Must actually use the app and show the real output
- Disclose AI generation and the paid partnership (FTC)
- Voice per [[Brand Guardrails]] - blunt, no hype, no outcome promises
- Deliver 9:16 raw + no music, so we can cut our own versions
