# Meta Ad Policy — WinkyPie risk areas

> Not legal advice. Verify each item against current Meta policy before launch and record the outcome.

## 1. Personal attributes — highest risk for us
Meta prohibits ad copy that **implies knowledge of** a person's personal attributes, including relationship status.

| Don't | Do |
|---|---|
| "Still single?" | "Built to get picked." |
| "Your dating profile isn't working" | "First photo. First chance." |
| "Tired of no matches?" | "One selfie. Every angle." |

Rule of thumb: address the **product**, not the viewer's situation. Second person about *his face*, not *his love life*.

## 2. Dating category
Meta's Dating restricted category requires written permission and applies to dating services. WinkyPie is a photo tool, not a dating service — but **verify before first launch** and record the answer in [[Configuration]].

## 3. Adult / suggestive content
Dating-adjacent creative gets scrutinised. Keep imagery clothed, non-suggestive, portrait-framed.

## 4. AI-generated content disclosure
Show AI results honestly. "Stylized AI representations - not a filter." Do not present a generated image as an unedited photograph.

## 5. Before/after
Meta restricts unexpected before/after imagery implying transformation of a person's body. Our before/after is *photo quality*, not *body change* — keep it framed that way, and prefer "same face, better photo" over any implied physical improvement.

## 6. Unrealistic outcomes
No "guaranteed matches". Overlaps with [[Claims & Proof]] and [[Brand Guardrails]].
