## Claims we may NOT make
- Any user count, download count, or star rating we haven't earned and can't evidence
- Any match/date/outcome promise ("guaranteed matches", "10x your matches")
- Any comparison to a named competitor without substantiation
- Any price on the web -> App Store listing is authoritative

## Claims we MAY make
- "Fresh launch - be among the first"
- Product-mechanic facts we can demo: one selfie, ~30 seconds, pose collection, face preserved
- "Stylized AI representations - not a filter" (required disclosure, not a claim)
