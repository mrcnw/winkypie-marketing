# App Store & Subscription Rules

Marketing-relevant only. Product/review rules live with engineering.

## Subscription marketing
- Free-trial promotion must state trial length and that it converts to paid, plus the cancellation path.
- The App Store listing is the authoritative price. Do not put dollar amounts in web or ad copy. -> [[Brand Guardrails]]

## Metadata
- No competitor brand names in the app name or subtitle (trademark risk).
- Keywords field is not user-visible but is still subject to review.
- Screenshots must show the actual app UI.

## AI content
Disclose that outputs are AI-generated. Do not imply photographic authenticity.

## Checklist before any campaign
- [ ] Trial terms in creative match the App Store listing exactly
- [ ] Cancellation path mentioned wherever the trial is promoted
- [ ] No price stated outside the App Store
