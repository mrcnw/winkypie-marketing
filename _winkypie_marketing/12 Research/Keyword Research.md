## Brand terms — legal check first
Dating-app brand names (`hinge photos`, `tinder pictures`) draw high-intent traffic but carry trademark risk in App Store metadata. **Bidding** on them in ASA is a separate question from **using** them in metadata. Get a decision and record it here before either.

## Method
1. App Store search autocomplete — type the seed, record suggestions. Free, and it reflects real queries.
2. Check which competitors rank for each term. -> [[Competitor Landscape]]
3. ASA **Discovery** campaigns mine terms we'd never guess — promote winners to exact match.
4. Re-run monthly. Category vocabulary shifts fast.
