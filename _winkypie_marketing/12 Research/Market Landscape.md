## The market we're in
Two markets overlap on us:
1. **Dating app users** — the demand. Big, mature, well-documented.
2. **AI photo generation** — the supply-side category. New, crowded, fast-moving.

We sell into (1) using (2). Our positioning wins if we look like a *dating* product, not an *AI* product. -> [[Positioning]]
