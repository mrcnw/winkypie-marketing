# Research Overview

Research is not a phase — it's the input that keeps [[Meta Ads Process.canvas|the loop]] fed. Our whole proof strategy is **research, not testimonials**, so this folder is load-bearing: if a stat isn't sourced here, it can't appear in an ad.

## What lives here
| Note | Purpose | Feeds |
|---|---|---|
| [[Studies & Stats]] | Citable third-party numbers | [[Claims & Proof]], A1 ads, `/methodology` |
| [[Voice of Customer]] | Raw user quotes, unedited | [[Hooks & Angles]] |
| [[Market Landscape]] | Market size, trends, adjacent categories | [[Positioning]] |
| [[Keyword Research]] | What people actually search | [[ASO]], [[Apple Search Ads]] |
| `Sources/` | One note per source, with the actual citation | [[Studies & Stats]] |
| `Interviews/` | User conversations | [[ICP & Personas]] |
| `Notes/` | Everything else | — |

## Division of labour
- **Here** = what's true about the world (audience, market, evidence).
- **[[Competitor Landscape|04 Competitors]]** = what rivals are doing.
- **[[06 Analytics/Funnel & North Star|06 Analytics]]** = what's true about *our* numbers.

Don't put competitor ads here, and don't put our performance data here.

## The one rule
**A number without a source is not research — it's a liability.** Every figure gets a row in [[Studies & Stats]] with a link and a date checked, or it never leaves this folder. -> [[Brand Guardrails]]

## Cadence
| When | What |
|---|---|
| Weekly (Tue) | [[Swipe Method]] mining → [[Voice of Customer]] |
| Monthly | One new source added to [[Studies & Stats]] |
| Quarterly | [[Market Landscape]] refresh |
| Before any new claim | Source it here first |
