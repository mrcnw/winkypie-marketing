## Sourcing standard
1. Find the **primary** source. A marketing blog citing "studies show" is not a source.
2. Read the actual finding. Check the population, sample size, and year.
3. Phrase our version so it **cannot overstate** the finding.
4. Add the row here **and** the `/methodology` entry, same wording.
5. Re-check links quarterly — dead link means the claim comes down.

## Never
Our own user counts, ratings, or results — until we genuinely have them and can evidence them. "Fresh launch — be among the first" is the honest alternative.
