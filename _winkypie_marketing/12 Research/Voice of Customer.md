## Language bank
Words *he* uses, that we should use back. Not marketing words.

| His word | Our word (kill it) |
|---|---|
| pics | imagery |
| looks weird | inauthentic representation |
| getting skipped | low engagement |

## Process
1. Paste the quote verbatim, with a link.
2. Only later, in a separate column, turn it into a hook.
3. Never merge steps 1 and 2 — polishing at capture time is how you lose the insight.
