# Keyword Map

The leader's vocabulary, what we may take from it, and the App Store fields it implies.
Source: the 42 live ads on the [[ROAST]] page (read sorted by impressions, 2026-09-01), the
other proven advertisers' hooks ([[SWAY]], [[Charmd]], [[ReGen]], [[TruShot]]), and their
store listings. Ad-hunting keywords (what to type into the Ad Library) live in
[[Swipe Method]]; this file is about the *language*.

## The leader's vocabulary (ROAST, by observed frequency)

| Word / frame | How they use it | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **photos** | "it's the photos, not your face" — the category's master reframe | In nearly every ad |
| **matches** | the outcome unit: "20 matches by Friday", "10x the matches" | Always a story number, not a promised rate |
| **swipe / swiped** | the moment of judgment: "she swiped left before your bio" | Matches our "Be the right swipe." |
| **free quiz · 2 minutes** | the risk-free first step, in almost every CTA | Their funnel entry; ours is the free photo check |
| **profile** | "profile optimizer", "profile review" — their category noun | We are not a review product; use sparingly |
| **optimized / unoptimized** | "You're not ugly. You're unoptimized." | Personal-attributes framing — banned for us |
| **gatekeeping** | "he was gatekeeping roast the whole time" | Social-currency frame, borrowable third-person |
| **724,000+ guys** | social proof in copy | We have no numbers yet — cannot copy (guardrail 2) |
| **the game / the whole game** | "photos are the whole game and nobody told you" | Locker-room register, on-voice for us |

Category vocabulary from the other proven ads: "AI dating photos" (ReGen, Charmd, GetDates),
"no photographer" (ReGen — collides with our locked line "Pro photos. No photographer."),
"look like you / looks completely real" (Charmd, TruShot — the trust objection said out
loud), "invisible" (TruShot "You're Not Invisible. Your Photos Are."; Charmd subtitle "Stop
Feeling Invisible"), "get more dates / matches" (everyone).

## What we take vs what we ban

| Take (rewritten third-person, product-led) | Ban (and why) |
|---|---|
| The **"it's the photos" reframe** — e.g. "The first photo does all the work." | "You're not ugly / unoptimized", "Struggling to get a girlfriend?" — Meta personal-attributes rule, PRODUCT.md §11 |
| **"No photographer"** — already ours, locked line | Any match-rate number ("10x the matches", "3x more") — invented stat |
| **Free-first-step CTA** — ours is stronger: "It checks your photo before it costs you anything." | User counts / "724,000+ guys" — no invented proof |
| **Story-shaped copy** (coworker, friend, weekend) in third person | Fake-persona pages ("Ethan Park", "Confessions of a Dater") — deception |
| "Looks like you" said as product truth — "Looks pro. Still you." | "Undetectable AI" (UnrealPhotos) — anti-thesis of guardrail 5 |
| Lowercase UGC register for ad copy tests | Fake countdowns, "gatekept secret" urgency mechanics |

## Our App Store fields (proposal — final ASO ships via the release checklist in `dream-pie`)

The delisted "AI Dating Photos: Get Matches" freed the exact-match category title; ReGen
holds "AI Dating Photos" as a live subtitle. Fields are proposals bounded by PRODUCT.md §11
— no counts, no rates, no second-person attributes:

| Field | Proposal | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Title | `WinkyPie: AI Dating Photos` | The category phrase with search volume and a dead former owner; brand first |
| Subtitle | `Pro photos. No photographer.` | Locked brand line, keyword-bearing (photos, photographer) |
| Keyword field candidates | `dating,photos,tinder,hinge,bumble,profile,picture,selfie,pose,headshot,matches,ai` | Union of the niche's proven vocabulary; dedupe against title/subtitle at submission |
| Never in metadata | match multipliers, user counts, "best", competitor names | Guardrails + App Store rules |
