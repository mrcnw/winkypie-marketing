# Competitor Landscape

Research date: 2026-09-01, second sweep same day (added GIO, SWAY, Charmd, Retake, Lensa,
Dawn, Softbox/Prequel checks). One file per competitor in `competitors/`. Ad data from live
Meta Ad Library scans (US, active; page views where available) done the same day.

Their vocabulary — what [[ROAST]] ranks for, what we may take from it, and the App Store
fields it implies for us — is in [[Keyword Map]].

## The gap, in one sentence

**Every live rival either demands 4–20 selfies, restyles old photos instead of making the
shot you posed for, charges before showing anything, or isn't about dating at all — no live
product turns one coached selfie into dating photos with a free check before any money
leaves your pocket.**

The one app that claimed "1 selfie → 10+ dating photos" ([[AI Dating Photos Get Matches (dead)]])
was delisted with its whole developer account. In the *dating* niche the claim is ownerless —
but generic one-photo products exist ([[GIO]], [[Pose AI]]), so the claim needs the coaching
+ free-check combination around it.

## Differentiator scoreboard (PRODUCT.md §5 vs reality — updated after sweep 2)

| Claimed differentiator | Still true? | Who breaks it |
|---|---|---|
| One selfie, no training | **✗ As a headline** | [[GIO]] (7.5M installs) and [[Pose AI]] both take one photo. Dating-specific one-selfie is still open |
| Free pre-generation check | **✓ Unique** — nobody checks the *input selfie* before charging | Closest: GetDates' watermarked previews (post-generation), Fotto/MatchShot audits of existing photos |
| Pose coaching before the shot | **✓ Unique** | Nobody. ReGen sells the opposite ("no guesswork") |
| Selfie-mirroring workflow | **✓ Unique** | Nobody — GIO/Retake restyle an old photo; we coach a new one |
| Upload your own reference | **✗ Not unique** | Photo AI ("Copycat"), TruShot ("recreate a photo from Instagram/Pinterest") |
| Dating-specific male curation | Partly | ROAST/TruShot/GetDates market to men; none ships coached, dating-curated *poses* |

**Creative implication (sharpened):** the unique, defensible combination is
**coached pose + mirrored selfie + free check before you pay**. "One selfie" and "copy any
photo" are supporting mechanics with live counter-examples — never the headline.

## Universal bad-review themes across every paid rival

1. **"Doesn't look like me"** — #1 everywhere (ROAST, Photo AI, ReGen, TruShot, Pose AI,
   SWAY, GIO — incl. ethnicity drift, Retake, Lensa-era memory).
2. **Pay before you see anything** — ReGen $50 blind, TruShot no sample, Umax blurred, GIO
   hidden limits after payment.
3. **Billing traps** — weekly subs, silent renewals, cancellation mazes, sub+credits
   double-dipping (GIO, SWAY, Charmd, Remini 1.4★ Trustpilot).
4. Few usable photos per dollar.

Our counters are shipped product facts (PRODUCT.md §5): pose-mimic likeness, free
pre-flight check, Apple-managed billing with trial notification.
