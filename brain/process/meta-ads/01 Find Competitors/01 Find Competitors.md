# 01 · Find Competitors

Checklist: [[01 TODO]] · Drawing: [[Process.excalidraw|Process]]

## Goal
A written map of who else sells "better photos of you" to the same man, what they charge,
what they promise, and the one thing none of them can say that WinkyPie can.

## Process
1. **Cast wide, then cut.** App Store search ("AI headshot", "dating photos", "profile
   picture AI"), Reddit (r/dating_advice, r/Tinder, r/OnlineDating), Google, and the
   "similar apps" rail on each App Store listing.
2. **One file per competitor** in `competitors/`. Same headings every time, so the
   files can be read side by side. A competitor with a half-filled file is worse than none —
   fill it or drop it.
3. **Ads before words.** What a competitor *runs* tells you more than its landing page.
   Meta Ad Library is public: search the page name, filter to active. Note how long an ad
   has been running — long-running ads are working ads.
4. **Mine the bad reviews.** One-star reviews are a list of unmet promises. Each one is a
   candidate angle for us — and a candidate objection we must pre-empt.
5. **Position against the category, not the feature list.** WinkyPie's claimed edge is one
   selfie, ~30 seconds, face and build preserved. Verify each competitor actually fails
   at that before claiming it. If one of them matches us, that is the most important
   finding in this step — write it in bold.
6. **Do not copy.** The output is a gap, not a swipe file. Swipes belong in
   [[02 How To Find A Good Ad]].

## Done when
- 6–8 competitor files exist in `competitors/`, each with pricing, turnaround,
  selfies required, hook, rating and at least one live-ad reference.
- `Competitor Landscape.md` holds the comparison table and the differentiation call.
- The gap is written as one sentence a stranger could understand.

## Output
- `Competitor Landscape.md`
- `competitors/<Name>.md` — one per competitor
- `Keyword Map.md` — the leader's vocabulary, what we take from it, and our App Store fields

## Notes
Watch out for: competitors that look identical but serve LinkedIn headshots, not dating.
Different buyer, different price tolerance, not our competition. Say so explicitly rather
than padding the list.
