# Photo AI (photoai.com)

**The famous indie generalist — big brand, zero paid ads, and the one competitor that
already has a reference-photo feature.** Pieter Levels' web-only generator; dating is one
vertical among many (Tinder/Bumble/Hinge packs).

## Facts

| Field | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Platform | **Web only** — iOS app existed, killed ("easier to focus on the website") | [FAQ](https://photoai.com/faq) |
| Pricing | Subs: $19/mo Starter (explicitly "low quality, low likeness"), $49 Pro, $99 Max, $199 Ultra | [pricing](https://photoai.com/pricing), seen 2026-09-01 |
| Selfies required | 5–15, FAQ recommends ~20 varied photos | [photoai.com](https://photoai.com), FAQ |
| Turnaround | Training 90 s (top tiers) to 2 h (lower); ~15 s per photo | FAQ |
| Hook | "🔥 Fire your photographer" · Tinder page: "Get 10x More Matches" | [photoai.com](https://photoai.com), [/tinder](https://photoai.com/tinder) |
| Audience | Generic, both sexes; dating = one pack | photoai.com |
| Traction | $105–138k MRR (founder posts / IndieHackers, Nov 2025); 30.6M photos generated (self-reported) | [levels.io](https://levels.io/photoai-40870-line-index-php-105k-mo-revenue), [IndieHackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/photo-ai-by-pieter-levels-complete-deep-dive-case-study-0-to-132k-mrr-in-18-months-3a9a2b1579) |

## Meta Ad Library (checked live 2026-09-01, US, active)

**Runs no ads.** The ~18 active ads matching "photoai.com" are all from copycat pages
("AI Photo Enhancer") pushing an Android app Photo AI doesn't have — rip-offs riding the
brand. Growth is 100% organic via levelsio's ~600k X audience
([IndieHackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/photo-ai-by-pieter-levels-complete-deep-dive-case-study-0-to-132k-mrr-in-18-months-3a9a2b1579)).
**He is not competing for Meta inventory — his audience is not our cold traffic.**

## Bad-review themes

Trustpilot **2.2★, 61% one-star** ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/photoai.com)):

1. **Likeness failure** — "my face just wouldn't look how it is"; "cannot be used".
2. **Cancellation friction** — "impossible to cancel, had to go through Stripe".
3. Billing/refund anger, no support, paid features broken.
4. Quality **tiered by price** — the $19 tier is officially "low likeness". Paying more to
   look like yourself is a resentment machine.

## Where WinkyPie wins / loses

- **Win:** 1 selfie vs 5–20 + training wait; likeness not price-gated; iOS-native; free
  pre-flight check vs his written guidelines.
- **Lose:** brand gravity, price ladder for power users, breadth (video, packs, API).
- ⚠️ **"Copycat" feature: Photo AI ships upload-a-reference-photo since 2023**
  ([levels.io](https://levels.io/photoai-photorealistic-ai-photo-studio)). Our
  "upload your own reference" differentiator is NOT unique against him — never claim
  "only WinkyPie lets you copy any photo". "One selfie + coaching + free check" remains ours.
