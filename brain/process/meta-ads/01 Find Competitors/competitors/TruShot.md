# TruShot (trushot.app)

**The biggest of the web one-time-payment swarm, and the second active Meta advertiser in
the niche.** Web-only, one-time packs, men-coded marketing. Fresh ad push started
2026-08-28 — they are testing Meta right now, including advertorials from a fake persona page.

## Meta Ad Library (checked live 2026-09-01, US, active)

4 active ads, all started **2026-08-28** (fresh test, EU transparency tag = also running in EU):

- Page "Trushot App": long direct-response copy — "Upload 4 normal selfies… 100+
  dating-focused templates, without the stiff photographer look or the obvious AI face…
  under $1 per photo… If your matches don't improve within 14 days, you get your money
  back." Headline: **"You're Not Invisible. Your Photos Are."**
- Page **"Ethan Park"** — a persona page running first-person advertorials ("For two years,
  I thought dating apps were proving I just wasn't attractive enough…"). Story format,
  hundreds of words, ends in TruShot pitch. Note: running ads from a fake-person page is
  the kind of deception we will not copy; the *story structure* itself is worth studying in
  [[02 How To Find A Good Ad]].

## Bad-review themes

1. Output "nothing like the ones advertised, way worse and artificial" ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/trushot.app)).
2. Credit depletion — minimum 3 photos per generation, few usable ("2-3 usable" from a $50 pack).
3. Refund/support friction despite the guarantee.

## Where WinkyPie wins / loses

- **Win:** they have no app (web checkout vs App Store trust), no pose coaching, no
  pre-generation check, and their own pages can't agree whether you need 4 or 20 selfies.
  Template packs only vs our coached pose-mimic flow.
- **Lose:** one-time $29 entry beats a subscription on impulse-purchase psychology;
  "money back if matches don't improve" is a strong risk-reversal we can't match (we can't
  promise match outcomes at all).
- ⚠️ **They ship reference-photo recreation** ("recreate a photo you love from Instagram or
  Pinterest") — second competitor after Photo AI with it. Never claim ours is unique.
