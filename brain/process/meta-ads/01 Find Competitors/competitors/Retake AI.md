## Bad-review themes

3. Likeness distortion — "Changes your face to be not even you", "looked like a clown".

## Relevance to WinkyPie

- Different mechanic (fix your existing photos vs make the photo you never had) and
  different buyer coding — but it competes for the same "look better in photos" wallet and
  proves weekly-sub AI photo apps monetize at $1M+/mo.
- **Threat scenario:** with 12-photo models, a generation engine and a $1M+/mo ad machine,
  a "Dating pack" from Codeway would flood the niche instantly. Same watch-condition as
  [[Remini]].
- No pose catalog, no coaching, no pre-check, no external reference upload — verified absent.
