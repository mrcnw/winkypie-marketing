# Charmd / GetDates (Echelon Digital Inc.)

**The most WinkyPie-shaped clone — weak execution, but ads that have survived six months and
one mechanic to watch: watermarked previews before payment.** One company, two brands:
charmd.ai ("AI wingman", gender-neutral) and getdates.ai (men-only photo funnel). Android
package `com.echelon.getdates` proves the rebrand.

## Meta Ad Library (checked live 2026-09-01, US, active)

**13 active ads, alive since 2026-03-01/03-11 (~6 months)** — second-longest-running set in
the niche after SWAY. Two pages: "Charmd" + persona/content page **"Confessions of a Dater"**.
App-install ads (iOS). Hooks:

- "You're not ugly. Your photos are just terrible. 😬 … Charmd uses AI to generate profile
  photos that look completely real. 🚫 No fake filters. 🚫 No weird AI look."
- "Your photos are killing your matches. Charmd uses AI to fix that in minutes. No studio.
  No cringe. Just more right swipes." → "Better Photos. More Matches."
- "Stop guessing what works. Our AI analyzes your bio and photos…" → "Is Your Profile
  Underperforming?" (the compliance-safe variant).

Six months of spend against 35 ratings says the funnel converts to installs but the product
doesn't retain — their ads are better than their app.

## Bad-review themes

1. Unclear pricing / credit-maze IAPs (App Store listing reviews).
2. Photo quality issues.
3. Charges continuing after cancellation.

## Where WinkyPie wins / loses

- **Win:** product quality gap is huge (3.9★/35 vs our shipped coached flow); their "looks
  like you" promise is undermined by their own reviews; one selfie vs 4–6 + face-model
  training + paid retrain ($9.99!) when it fails.
- **Lose / watch:** **getdates.ai ships watermarked preview photos before payment, no card**
  — the closest anyone comes to our free pre-flight check, but post-generation. If they move
  it pre-generation, our uniqueness there narrows. Also: their 6-month ad set is a free
  library of proven niche hooks.
