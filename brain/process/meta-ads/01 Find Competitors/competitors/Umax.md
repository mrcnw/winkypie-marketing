# Umax (Umax — Become Hot)

**Not a photo generator — a looksmaxxing rating app. Competes for the same man's wallet
and insecurity, not for our feature set.** Blake Anderson (Cal AI). Sells diagnosis
("your jawline: 6.2") + self-improvement tips at $3.99/wk; no dating photos produced.

## Meta Ad Library (checked live 2026-09-01, US, active)

**Zero app ads** — "Umax" exact phrase returns only Yamaha UMAX golf carts. Consistent with
their stated strategy: paid ads are minor; the engine is ~$50/promo underground teenage
TikTok/IG creators ([Whop](https://whop.com/blog/looksmaxxing-blake-anderson/)). Their
distribution playbook is not reusable for Meta, but proves the male appearance-anxiety
audience converts at scale.

## Bad-review themes

1. Bait-and-paywall: selfies collected before the price reveal, results blurred.
2. Inconsistent ratings — same photo scores differently per scan.
3. Paid results never arrive / stuck loading.

## Relevance to WinkyPie

- Validates the wallet: the same 18–30 man pays weekly for "become more attractive".
- Their tone (ratings, "become hot", body-anxiety) is everything PRODUCT.md §9 forbids us —
  awe and empowerment, never insecurity. Umax is the anti-pattern of our voice.
- No overlap on features; keep on the watchlist only for if they ever ship photo generation.
