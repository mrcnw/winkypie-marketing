# Pose AI (pose.ai)

**The closest input-model twin: one selfie in, photos out — but generalist, both-gender,
and not buying Meta ads.** Web + iOS + Android studio app (400+ styles, AI video) with
dating packs. The only web-swarm member with a real App Store footprint.

## Meta Ad Library (checked live 2026-09-01, US, active)

## Bad-review themes

1. "Price points aren't worth the results", output "mediocre".
2. Likeness failure — faces "awkwardly pasted", "cartoony".
3. Trial-to-subscription billing complaints (caveat: some complaints belong to MWM's
   similarly named "Pose: AI Photo Video Generator" — naming collision).

## Where WinkyPie wins / loses

- **Win:** dating focus vs their 400-style buffet; pose coaching + free pre-flight check
  (they have neither — a "Prompt Builder" is not coaching); men-only curation; fresher app
  (theirs last updated 2025-08). No reference-photo upload on their dating product.
- **Lose:** they can say "one selfie" too — **the single competitor that neutralizes our
  input-count claim**, so our ads must lean on coaching + free check + dating curation, not
  "one selfie" alone. Cross-platform reach and AI-video breadth we don't have.
