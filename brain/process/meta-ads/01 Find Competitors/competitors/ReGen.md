# ReGen (ReGen: AI Dating Photos)

**The direct App Store rival that is actually buying Meta traffic — and being punished for
its paywall.** iOS-only, pose-library mechanic, heavy ad spend since July 2026, 3.2★ from a
$50-before-you-see-anything model. Bounty Studio, Inc.; released 2026-01-16.

## Meta Ad Library (checked live 2026-09-01)

**Heavy, disciplined spend — ~98 active ads** matching their page name (US view, checked
live 2026-09-01), start dates 2026-07-09 → 2026-08-08. Pattern: **one control primary text ×
many video variants** (0:18–0:55) — the classic scaling structure:

> "Your dating profile is only as good as your photos. ReGen turns 3–7 selfies into
> hundreds of realistic photos of you — the kind that actually get swipes. No photographer,
> no outfit planning, no asking a friend to take 200 shots at the park. Just upload and
> pick your favorites." → Install now.

Longest runner since 2026-07-14 (~7 weeks live). Also runs TikTok influencer #ad spots.

## Bad-review themes (1–2★, App Store)

1. **Hard paywall before any output** — "Cheapest plan is $50 up front and no way to even
   try it out". Dominant complaint.
2. Manipulative 10-minute onboarding before the price reveal.
3. Likeness/artifacts — "Long neck, weird facial expressions… 12 generic poses… ai slop".
4. Refund anger.

## Where WinkyPie wins / loses

- **Win:** every 1★ theme is our differentiator played straight: free pre-generation check
  vs $50 blind; 1 selfie vs 6 + avatar wait; coached pose-mimic vs "12 generic poses"; no
  reference-photo upload on their side. Their "no guesswork, system handles it" copy is the
  exact opposite of our coaching story — clean contrast for creative.
- **Lose:** six months of paid learnings and creative iteration ahead of us; polished video
  ad pipeline; credits + subscription hybrid monetization.
- Their control ad copy is the best current template of "what Meta approves" in this niche
  — third-person, product-led, no personal attributes. Save to [[02 How To Find A Good Ad]] swipes.
