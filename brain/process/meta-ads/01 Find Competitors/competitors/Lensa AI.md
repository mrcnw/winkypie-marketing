**Alive and maintained, but not a dating competitor: Magic Avatars is stylized art, ~98%
off its 2022 peak, and the app has repositioned as a general photo editor.** Checked because
the team asked; verdict: adjacent at most.

## Relevance to WinkyPie

- The cautionary tale of the avatar era: viral spike → likeness disappointment →
  monetization squeeze → decline. Dawn AI (Bending Spoons) is the same story, further along
  — 14 months without an update, ~1.8k downloads/mo, dismissed entirely.
- Useful for positioning: the market already learned "AI avatars ≠ usable photos". Our
  "Looks pro. Still you." line lands on soil Lensa salted.
- No pose catalog, no coaching, no pre-check, no reference upload.
