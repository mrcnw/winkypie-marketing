# ROAST (roast.dating)

**The category leader and the only serious paid-Meta player in dating AI photos.** Profile
review + AI photo generation for dating apps. Runs ads from two pages: **"Roast AI"** and a
second brand, **"Photoshoot Dating"** (photoshoot.dating). Our most important competitor.

## Meta Ad Library (checked live 2026-09-01, US, active)

The biggest active spender in the niche. ~9 active ads matching "roast.dating" exact phrase
+ more under photoshoot.dating; ~84 total including inactive since May 2026.

- **Longest runner: since 2026-05-21** (library ID 1728951051762643) — "You're swiping on
  women you actually want to date. They're not swiping back. ROAST scans your profile…
  generates new photos with AI. Takes 2 minutes." 3+ months live = their proven winner.
- Hooks in rotation (started 2026-08-17): "You're not ugly. You're unoptimized." · "girls
  like her don't swipe on the best guy, they swipe on the best photos" · "the best-looking
  guy almost never wins on Tinder. the guy with the best photos does" · "It's the photos,
  not you." (Photoshoot Dating page) · coworker-gatekeeping story with "724,000+ guys" proof.
- Formula: lowercase UGC-style confession/story + "it's the photos, not your face" reframe +
  free 2-min quiz CTA. Video 0:28–1:16.
- Note: their copy openly violates Meta's personal-attributes rule ("You're not ugly") —
  they eat the rejection risk; we must not copy that framing (PRODUCT.md §11).

## Bad-review themes (= our candidate angles)

1. **Likeness failure** — "body they used was a small skinny guy, looked nothing like me";
   "cartoonish", "plastic" ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/roast.dating)).
2. **Billing traps** — trial converting silently, $70 surprise charges, fake countdown
   timers on pricing page ([SwipeStats](https://www.swipestats.io/blog/roast-dating-app-review)).
3. **Inconsistent scoring** — same photo rated 60% then 100%.
4. **Bait funnel** — free quiz, then forced payment to see results.

## Where WinkyPie wins / loses

- **Win:** 1 selfie vs their 4–10; no training wait; free pre-generation check vs their
  paid reveal; photos stay on the phone. Their #1 complaint (doesn't look like me) is our
  pose-mimic argument: the composition is real because you posed for it.
- **Lose:** they own the "profile review" wedge, massive social proof (724k users), a
  years-old funnel, and an SEO/blog moat. They can say numbers we cannot ([[Competitor Landscape]]).
- No pose coaching, no reference-photo upload, no pre-flight check — verified absent on
  their sales pages 2026-09-01.

**Read for us:** the leader's proven structure is third-person stories about *him* (our
personal-attributes rule, for free), a free first step, "2 minutes", and now AI photos as
the promise — our core. Seven months of "50% OFF today only" says the funnel needed a
discount to close: their margin, not their creative, is the soft spot. The "yacht / 911"
deception angle is banned for us (PRODUCT.md §11.5).
