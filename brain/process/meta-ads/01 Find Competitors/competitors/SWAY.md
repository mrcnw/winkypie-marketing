# SWAY (Sway AI: Dating App Assistant)

**The longest-living ad in the niche and the closest full-bundle rival on iOS.** Profile
review (scored screenshots) + photo analysis + AI photo generation + bios + Hinge prompts.
Easy Mode Ventures LLC. Their Meta ad has run since **2026-02-17 (~196 days)** — by the
longevity rule, the single most proven creative in the category.

## Meta Ad Library (checked live 2026-09-01, US, active)

The record holder: ad live since **2026-02-17** —

~6.5 months of continuous spend = profitable creative. Note it opens with a
personal-attributes question ("Struggling to get a girlfriend?") — they eat the policy risk;
we don't copy that (PRODUCT.md §11).

## Bad-review themes

1. AI photos don't look like the user — distorted hands, wrong features
   ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/6502842382/sway-ai-dating-app-assistant/reviews)).
2. Paid $15 photo pack never delivered; refunds ignored.
3. À-la-carte resentment — photo generator costs extra on top of the subscription.

## Where WinkyPie wins / loses

- **Win:** we generate as the core product (their photos are a bolted-on upsell with the
  category's likeness problem); one coached selfie vs re-styling old photos; one price, no
  à-la-carte maze; free check before payment.
- **Lose:** they own the "review my profile" entry point — a cheaper first click than "make
  me photos" — and they've had a working Meta funnel since February. Their review-first
  funnel is the model ROAST proved; ours starts at the photo.
- No pose coaching, no pre-generation check, no reference upload — verified absent.
