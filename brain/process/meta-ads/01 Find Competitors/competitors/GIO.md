# GIO (GIO — AI Photoshoot Generator, by Prequel Inc.)

**The biggest one-selfie competitor — and the answer to "what is Gio / Prequel".** Prequel
Inc.'s AI photo-studio app: upload ONE photo, pick from a styles catalog, get headshots and
portraits in seconds. Dating is mentioned only euphemistically ("a connection seeker wanting
to stand out on social apps"), but the product covers our promise closer than anyone at scale.

## Bad-review themes

1. **Sub + credits double-charging** — "Paid for the subscription… have to keep paying more";
   "$6.93 advertised but also an additional $32" ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/6449296449/gio-ai-portrait-photo-editor/reviews)).
2. Cancellation near-impossible ("There is NO LINK!! They are thieves!").
3. **Likeness failures incl. ethnicity drift** — "turned a Hispanic image into a white guy".
4. Undisclosed limits — ~2 photoshoots/week even on pro.

## Where WinkyPie wins / loses

- **Win:** dating-specific curation vs their headshot-first styles; pose coaching + mimic
  flow (they restyle the photo you upload — outfit/background swap — not a posed shot you
  took for the scene); free pre-flight check vs paywall-then-limits; honest pricing story
  against their sub+credits maze.
- **Lose:** scale (7.5M installs), speed ("in seconds"), TikTok distribution muscle, and
  they neutralize "one selfie" as a claim — **with Pose AI, that's two live one-photo
  products.** "One selfie" can be our mechanic, never our headline differentiator.
- Styles catalog ≠ pose catalog: theirs swaps looks on the same base photo; no coaching, no
  reference upload, no pre-check — verified absent in listing and reviews.
