# Remini (Bending Spoons)

**The 800-pound gorilla of the App Store search results — but it does not sell dating.**
Photo enhancer with an AI Photos generator bolted on; generic, both sexes, entertainment-led.
It will outrank us on "AI photos" searches and outspend us everywhere, so we must not fight
it on category terms — only on the dating wedge it ignores.

## Meta Ad Library (checked live 2026-09-01, US, active)

~55 active ads matching "Remini". Long-runners since 2026-05-28. **All entertainment hooks,
zero dating:** "Ever wondered how you'd look as a caricature?" · "New hairstyle in mind?
Try it with Remini first" · "See yourself as the other gender" · "Try the new Remini AI
Photobooth". Heavy paid UA at industrial scale (AppGrowing measured 1,500+ ads/day in 2024).
They buy volume with novelty, not intent.

## Bad-review themes

1. **Weekly-subscription trap** — Trustpilot ~1.4★, ~82% one-star: unauthorized recurring
   charges, failed cancellations, double-billing ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/remini.me)).
2. **Unwanted body edits** — "Why did AI give me a boob job" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/07/20/remini-tops-the-app-store-for-its-viral-ai-headshots-but-its-body-edits-go-too-far-some-say/)).
3. Output doesn't look like the user (official help article exists for it).
4. Ad-stuffed free tier, hidden paywalls.

## Where WinkyPie wins / loses

- **Win:** dating positioning (they have none), 1 selfie vs 8–12 + training, pose coaching
  and free pre-flight check (they have neither), honest male-focused curation vs novelty
  filters. Their pose selection is a built-in gallery — **no user-uploaded reference**.
- **Lose:** distribution, brand, price perception, App Store rank. If Remini ever ships a
  "Dating pack" they can flood the niche in a week (they shipped 30 packs in a week after
  Nano Banana). Watch for that.
