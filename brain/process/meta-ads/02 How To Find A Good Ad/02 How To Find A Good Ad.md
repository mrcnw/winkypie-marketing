# 02 · How To Find A Good Ad

Checklist: [[02 TODO]] · Canvas: [[Meta Ads.canvas|Meta Ads]]

## Goal
A repeatable, timeboxed method for sourcing ad hooks — so that "we have no ideas" is never
a valid reason for not shipping a creative. Plus a swipe file that is already stocked.

## Process
1. **30 minutes, once a week, same slot.** Longer sessions do not produce better hooks;
   skipped sessions produce none.
2. **Four sources, in this order:**
   - *Meta Ad Library* — active ads in our category. Duration is the signal: an ad running
     8+ weeks is spending money profitably.
   - *App Store reviews* — ours and competitors'. Verbatim customer language.
   - *Reddit and forums* — how men actually describe the problem when nobody is selling.
   - *Outside the category* — fitness, grooming, dating coaching. The best hooks are
     usually borrowed from an adjacent market, not from a direct competitor.
3. **Capture the mechanism, not the asset.** For every swipe write three lines:
   - what happens in the first 1.5 seconds
   - what tension it creates
   - what it asks the viewer to believe
   A swipe without those three lines is a screenshot, not research.
4. **Steal words, not footage.** The strongest hooks are sentences the customer already
   said. "Only photos of me are group shots and gym mirror selfies" is a hook. "Transform
   your dating life" is not.
5. **Score before producing.** A hook is worth a brief if it passes all four:

   | Test | Question |
   |---|---|
   | Specific | Would it make sense only for our ICP, or for any app? Generic = dead. |
   | Silent | Does it read with sound off, in the first 1.5 s? |
   | Honest | Can we say it without a fabricated stat or an outcome promise? |
   | Single | Does it test exactly one idea? |

6. **Kill on sight:** anything implying the viewer's dating status ("Still single?") —
   Meta's Personal Attributes policy rejects it. Anything with an invented number.
   Anything showing a before/after that reads as a different person.

## Done when
- The weekly slot is in the calendar and has happened at least twice.
- `Swipe Method.md` documents the ritual, the sources and the rubric.
- `swipe/` holds 20+ swipes, each with the three-line mechanism note.
- `Hooks And Angles.md` holds 10+ scored hooks ready to brief.

## Output
- `Swipe Method.md`
- `Hooks And Angles.md`
- `swipe/` — one file per swipe

## Notes
The failure mode here is collecting instead of extracting. A swipe file nobody scores is
a hoarding habit. Twenty scored swipes beat two hundred saved ones.
