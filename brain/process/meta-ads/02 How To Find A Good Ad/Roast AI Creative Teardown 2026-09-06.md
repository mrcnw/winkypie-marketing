## What the transcripts show that the copy does not

1. **The pitch changed in August 2026.** May–July ads sell a free two-minute profile
   *review*. Every August video sells *AI photos*, and the mechanism spoken aloud is a
   face swap: "generate aesthetic images of yourself using other guys' photos" (four
   listicles), "swap your face with theirs" (1579081293769974), "they're all AI… I swapped
   my face" (the five-variant set). Roast now advertises the thing WinkyPie makes. Read
   this with the family timeline in [[ROAST]].
2. **Deception is the promise, not a side effect.** "she thinks he drives a 911 / owns a
   yacht / went to Italy", "I don't care if you think it's cheating", "not proud of it",
   "game is game". Our honesty rule is the opposite position in the market, which makes it
   an angle: "Looks pro. Still you." ([[Hooks And Angles]]).
3. **The plug is one sentence, buried.** In the listicles the product occupies six of
   43–50 seconds and arrives after 60 % of the runtime; everything before it is content the
   viewer would watch anyway. Shape: content title → tips → one-sentence plug with an App
   Store card → back to content.
4. **Numbers said on camera:** "trained on like 700,000 dating profiles", "724,000+ guys",
   "820,000+ profiles improved" (landing page), "one AI photo got more matches in 48 hours
   than three months", "two matches a week to two a day". All self-reported or scripted.
   None is usable by us.
5. **Every voice is a man talking to men.** Women appear only blurred, as a DM list, or as
   "girls like her". No female imagery — the same rule as ours, for a different reason.
6. **Where the `<100` flags fall:** on the long ones (1:09–1:10 confession, interview,
   podcast). The families they replicate (five and four variants) are 0:37–0:50,
   picture-in-picture over a slideshow.
