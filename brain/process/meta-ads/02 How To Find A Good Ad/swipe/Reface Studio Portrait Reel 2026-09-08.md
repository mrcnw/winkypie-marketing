## Mechanism (the three lines from [[02 How To Find A Good Ad]])

- **First 1.5 s:** an ordinary woman talking in a small corner box, a magazine cover behind
  her. The gap between the two is the hook and it lands with the sound off — you do not need
  a word to know she is claiming the big picture is her.
- **Tension:** those portraits read as a paid studio day. She says she never left her room.
- **Asked to believe:** one selfie becomes ten magazine-grade portraits, in seconds, and it is
  still recognisably you.

## What we copy

1. **The result is always full-frame; the host is a 25 % inset.** There is no talking-head
   shot in 53 seconds — every frame is a portfolio image doing the selling while the voice
   explains. This is the opposite of the Roast listicles, where the product waits behind
   content ([[Roast AI Creative Teardown 2026-09-06]] §3), and it is cheaper to produce.
2. **"One photo in, a full photoshoot out."** The clearest input→output line anyone in this
   niche has said. Ours, in the same six-beat shape and with the dating context §9 requires:
   **"One selfie in. Photos for your profile out."** → score it in [[Hooks And Angles]].
3. **Product late, product brief.** The screen recording starts at 0:38 — 72 % of runtime —
   and runs five seconds: home → filter → upload. Same ratio as every Roast winner.
4. **The AI disclosure as an end-card footer.** Small grey line under the CTA, costs nothing,
   and it is exactly the honesty PRODUCT.md §11.5 asks of us. Reface ships it on a reel with
   121k views — it is not a conversion tax.
5. **The before shot is a real bad selfie.** Car park, hoodie, elevator mirror. Not a staged
   "bad" photo — which is what makes the after credible. Matches our `bad-photos/` set.
6. **A four-sentence voiceover carries 13 seconds** because the visual changes every 1.5 s.
   Write to that cadence, not to a paragraph.

## What we never copy

- **The casting.** Female subject start to finish, in an ad for a product whose own catalog
  has "Men's Studio Portrait" and "He in Black & White" rows visible on screen. Guardrail 1 /
  PRODUCT.md §11.7. The male version of this exact ad is one casting change away — that is the
  brief, not a translation of this one.
- **"a pack of your heartbreaking shots"** — second-person, promises an effect on other
  people. Meta personal-attributes risk (PRODUCT.md §11) and an outcome claim we do not make.
- **"10 stunning magazine style shots"** — a count. Ours is backend-managed and changes
  without a release; guardrail 7 says never state one without a live check.
- **Blue captions burned over the image.** Illegible on a bright frame. Ours stay black on a
  white box at the bottom.
