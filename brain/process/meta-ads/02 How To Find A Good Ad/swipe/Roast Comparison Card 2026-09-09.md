## Mechanism

- **First 1.5 s:** a category-news frame ("how guys are *actually* …, 2025"), not a product
  claim. It reads as a list you might have missed, and the grid is legible before any word is.
- **Tension:** the time, the outcome and the money you already spend inside the dating app
  buy you nothing. Someone else has a cheaper route.
- **Asked to believe:** a free two-minute quiz tells you what is wrong. Nothing on the card
  asks him to believe anything about the paid product — that argument is moved to the landing.

**The three rows are three dimensions, and both columns answer the same one:** row 1 time
(hours → 2 minutes, scrambled across the grid), row 2 knowledge (ignored, no idea why → know
exactly what's wrong), row 3 money (Tinder Gold → free). Read across, not down. That is the
whole trick of the format and it is what makes it scan in under two seconds.

**The ✓ column describes the free step, not the product.** Knowledge, speed, price — three
restatements of "Take the free quiz", and the money row sits directly above the button. Their
paid product (AI photos) is not on the card at all.

**The ✗ column is one coherent thing:** where his time and money go *inside Tinder today*.
Not a competitor, not another app. That coherence is why the Tinder flame works as a column
label for them — it is the wallet they are arguing with.

## Copy this

1. **One dimension per row, answered by both columns.** The single reason the card reads fast.
2. **The free/cheap row last**, touching the CTA.
3. **A category frame as the hook**, not a product claim.
4. **Three rows, three to five words each.** Six bullets is the whole ad.
5. **A ✗ column that is one thing**, nameable in two words.

## Never copy this

| Theirs | Why |
|---|---|
| The Tinder flame as the column label | A third-party mark in paid creative. Match Group runs brand-rights protection; there is no comparative purpose in a competitor's *logo* even where naming them would be lawful. See [[Comparison Card Bullets]] § blockers |
| "Getting ignored" | Asserts the viewer's results — the personal-attributes line PRODUCT.md §11 tells us not to cross. Roast eats that risk on nearly every creative; we do not |
| "Wasting money on Gold" | Same rule, plus a second Match Group mark, plus a comparison we cannot honestly make: WinkyPie does not compete with a Boost |
| "Fix it in 2 minutes" | A timing claim. Ours would be unsourced — median generation time was never measured (PRODUCT.md §13.5) |
| "How guys are actually getting dates in **2025**" | Fine as a shape; the year-stamp dates the asset and forces a reprint every January |
