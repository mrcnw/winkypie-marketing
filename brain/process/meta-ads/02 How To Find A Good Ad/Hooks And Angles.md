# Hooks And Angles

Candidate hooks scored with the [[02 How To Find A Good Ad]] rubric — **Specific** (our ICP
only?), **Silent** (reads sound-off in 1.5 s?), **Honest** (no invented stat, no outcome
promise?), **Single** (one idea?). Sources: the proven ads in [[Winning Ads 2026-09-02]],
competitor bad reviews (customer language), and PRODUCT.md's locked lines. Chosen → a
campaign in step 03; Backup → swap-in material; Dropped/Banned → recorded so they stop
coming back.

## Chosen (the five campaigns)

Revised 2026-09-02 after re-reading the winners: campaign 2 moved to the diagnostic-question
pattern (the mechanic of the niche's biggest ad), campaign 3 to a confession-story opener
(the proven UGC shape) with coaching as the twist.

## Dropped — and why (so they stay dropped)

| Candidate | Why |
|---|---|
| "You're not ugly. Your photos are just terrible." (Charmd, 185 d proof) | Fails Honest/guardrails: second-person appearance = Meta personal-attributes violation, and body-insecurity is an emotion we do not use (PRODUCT.md §9) |
| "Struggling to get a girlfriend?" (SWAY, 197 d proof) | Same rule — implies relationship status |
| "We are looking for 30 guys…" casting call (Roast, 107 d) | Fails Honest today: fake scarcity/selection we cannot fulfil. **Conditional candidate since 2026-09-02:** allowed only as a *genuine* beta programme — 30 real free months, real feedback, permission to quote — written up as [[WP_P2_STATIC_betacall]] (wave two). Roast's wording "get zero matches" stays banned: it implies the viewer's dating performance (personal attributes) |
| "10x / 3x / 9× more matches" | Invented multipliers — banned (§11.1, §8) |
| "724,000+ guys already did it" class social proof | No real numbers yet — banned until 50+ reviews exist (§11.1) |
| "New week. New pose." variety angle | Depends on live catalog cadence we haven't verified; weakest pillar (12%). Parked, not dead |
| Long-copy advertorials from persona pages (TruShot/Charmd) | The pages are fake people — deception we don't do. The *story structure* survives inside campaign copy, third-person |
| Fake-friend story ("His coworker joined Hinge the same week…" — Roast's #1 by impressions, 131 d) | Invented social proof (§11.1). Unlocks only as a **real** user story once real reviews exist (50+ threshold) |
| ICP callout ("If you're between 25 and 34 and can't get more than 1 match a week" — Roast, 119 d) | Textbook personal attributes: age + implied dating performance. Roast eats the rejection risk; we don't |
