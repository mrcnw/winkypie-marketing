## What the winners have in common (mechanism, per the [[Swipe Method]] rubric)

1. **First 1.5 s:** a claim about *photos*, not the product ("your photos are killing your
   matches", "same face, 10x"). Product name arrives late or never in the hook.
2. **Tension:** blame-shift — it's not your face / not the algorithm / not Tinder; the
   thing between you and matches is fixable and cheap.
3. **Asked to believe:** a specific, small first step (free 2-min quiz · upload 4 photos ·
   see what's wrong) — never "buy photos".
4. **Format:** the longest runners are low-fi — phone screenshots, memes, printed notes,
   UGC-style talking video. Polished product video is the *newest* material (Photoshoot
   Dating's 16-day wave), i.e. still unproven.
5. The single biggest-reach ad (#1) is the **compliance-safe variant** — proof the niche
   does not need personal-attributes violations to scale. Our guardrails are not a handicap.

## What we do NOT copy

"You're not ugly" second-person appearance framing · "Struggling to get a girlfriend?" ·
fake-persona pages (Confessions of a Dater, Ethan Park) · invented multipliers ("10x the
matches") · "724,000+ guys" class social proof until we have real numbers.
