### Cover

Creator brief. Five ads, five scripts. For UGC creators · Round one · 2026-09-02.

| | |

This document is the brief for a disclosed, scripted spokesperson video. It is not a

### 0 · Read this first

**What WinkyPie is.** An iPhone app that turns one selfie into a dating-profile photo that
looks like a photographer took it. You pick a pose from a catalog, the app tells you how to
stand, you take one selfie, and the AI renders *you* — your face, your build — in that pose
and that place. It does two things other AI photo apps don't: it **coaches the pose** before
you shoot, and it **checks your selfie for free** before anything costs you.

**Who is watching.** A man, 22–40, on Tinder, Hinge or Bumble, whose first profile photo is
a group shot or a mirror selfie. He thinks AI photos look fake and doubts one would still
look like him. You talk to him as one guy to another — about photos and the app. Never to a
woman, and never about anyone's love life.

**The screens you will show** (the real app on your phone, filmed over your shoulder — never
a mock-up, never a screenshot we sent you):

1. Poses grid — pick a pose
2. Pose card — the coaching, then "Add your photo"
3. Photo check — green tick, match %
4. Generating
5. Result — with "Pose used / Photo used"

**What we are ordering.** Five scripts, one video each. Every video tests one idea — its
first line. You may be asked for one script or several; each video stands on its own. It
runs as our ad from our page. You post nothing.

**Step zero — before you film (required).**

1. Install WinkyPie from the App Store: `apps.apple.com/us/app/winkypie/id6757441777`.
   Start the free trial — it covers everything you need for this. Do not pay for anything.
   If you need more than the trial gives, message us and we cover it.
2. Pick a pose, follow the coaching, take your selfie, let the check run, generate one photo.
3. Send us a screenshot of your result screen — the one with **Pose used / Photo used** —
   **before your shoot date.** No screenshot, no shoot.

Why: in every script you talk about using the app. US advertising law (the FTC endorsement
rules) requires that a person shown using a product has really used it. The photo you show
on camera must be your own result, generated from your own selfie — never something we send
you. The trial renews after 3 days unless cancelled in your Apple subscriptions; that is
your call after the shoot.

**How to read a script.**

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| LOCKED | Say it word for word. Usually the first line and one line in the middle. These are the lines we are testing |
| Your words | The meaning is fixed, the wording is yours. Talk, don't read. Short sentences |
| INSERT | Leave a clean 1–2 s pause; we cut the app screen in there. Also film that screen over your shoulder |
| A / B | A selfie moment — film it twice, see below |
| We add | The right column of every beat sheet is added by us in editing. Deliver clean footage: no captions, subtitles, stickers, music or logos |

**Selfie moments — film them twice.** Wherever a script has you take the selfie, shoot the
beat two ways and deliver both. **Variant A** — as written: you mirror the pose and take
the selfie on camera. **Variant B** — phone in hand, you look at the pose card and the
coaching, then cut; you are never seen photographing yourself, the selfie is implied. Why:
Meta's ad rules for this category may not allow a person visibly photographing themselves.
We choose the master; you deliver both.

**The rules — never, in any video.**

- **Claim results.** No matches, dates, likes, "it worked", "more replies" — and no numbers
  of any kind. The story ends at the photo.
- **Talk about dating life or relationship status** — yours or the viewer's. You talk about
  your photos and the app.
- **Say a price or any money amount** — not the app's, not what photographers cost. The
  only allowed line is "three days free, cancel anytime".
- **Say how many poses there are.**
- **Call it a filter, a face swap or an editor**, or suggest the photo shows a different
  person. It is AI, it renders you, and we label it as AI on screen. Don't pretend otherwise.
- **Name other apps** or services.
- **Show anyone but yourself.** No women on screen, in the photos you show, or in the poses
  you pick.
- **Invent proof** — "my friend tried it", "studies show", ratings, reviews. Don't say
  "review" or "honest review" on camera. This is a disclosed ad.
- **Visible logos, music, captions.**

**Tone.** Like telling a friend what you found. Short sentences. Calm and confident. No hype
words, no "insane", no shouting, no sales voice. At the reveal we want a real reaction —
"wait, that's me?" — surprise, not disbelief. Being a bit sceptical at the start is fine;
that is the story.

**Look.** Natural light, a real room or street, phone at eye level, your face big in the
frame, vertical. Clean audio: no wind, no music, no TV. Plain clothes, no logos. Low-fi is
the point — it should look like you shot it, because you did.

**Questions.** Message us on Billo before you film, not after. Two things we always say yes
to: swapping a middle line for your own phrasing, and sending the first 3 seconds of your
hook early so we can tell you whether the delivery works.

generate synthetic (AI) media of you without a separate written agreement and fee.

What we check the day it lands:

- [ ] Locked lines said word for word.
- [ ] Every beat present, in order.
- [ ] The first line lands inside 1.5 s — no preamble.
- [ ] Real app screens, your own result, readable on camera.
- [ ] No results claimed, no numbers, no prices, no other apps, no one else on screen, nothing about dating life.

- [ ] Master + two alt openings + selfie variant B + app screens + b-roll + raw, named by ad code.

One last thing: the video that wins is never the most polished one. It is the one where the
first three seconds feel like a real person saying something true. Aim there.
