# 04.1 · Outsource The Shoot

Checklist: [[04.1 TODO]] · Drawing: [[Process.excalidraw|Process]]

## Goal
Every campaign brief from [[03 Choose Videos And Five Campaigns]] turned into a delivered,
spec-compliant creative by an external creator — without us holding a camera. The industry
name for this is **UGC creator sourcing** (also "creative-as-a-service" for the agency
variant) — that's the term to search and to put in job posts.

## Process
1. **Split the work into three lanes.** Round one: the two statics and the pose→result piece
   are design-only (in-house); the screen demo is our own app capture; only
   `WP_P1_UGC_coached` needs an external human. Lane three is **synthetic and runs in
   parallel, not before**: 3–5 host-style angle lines are generated as AI *host* clips
   (Higgsfield MCP, real app screenshots composited) and tested cheaply — they feed the DEMO
   campaign and the backup hooks, and give the creator an animatic reference. They cannot
   test the confession hook, which is first-person and human-only; the Billo package's 3–5
   creators are that test. Do not hold the shoot for the AI lane — see
   `AI Production Platforms.md`. The AI lane never delivers the final UGC piece: a generated
   presenter is a host, not a user (§11.1).
2. **Pick the platforms once, in writing.** Human creators: the comparison and the pick live
   in `Production Platforms.md`. Synthetic: `AI Production Platforms.md`. Criteria that
   matter for us, in order: can we choose the *specific* creator (male 25–40, US English) ·
   full **paid-ads usage rights included** · accepts a dating-app brief · price per video ·
   turnaround.
3. **Order with a package, not a chat.** The reusable order template is `Shoot Order.md`:
   the brief link, the beat sheet verbatim, the hook word-for-word, specs (9:16 master,
   sound-off legibility), the guardrail one-pager — `Production Guardrails.md`, which adds
   the two production-time risks (Meta dating policy, FTC bona fide use) on top of §11 — and
   links to reference ads (links, never files — their creatives are their IP). The same
   one-pager's Lane B section is the brief for every Higgsfield run.
4. **Rights before payment.** Full paid usage rights for Meta, in writing, plus the raw
   footage. A video without written ad rights cannot run — this is the most common gotcha
   on UGC marketplaces.
5. **QA against the brief, not against taste.** Accept when: every beat present, hook
   legible with sound off in 1.5 s, specs pass, §11 clean, §11.2 disclosure on the reveal.
   Reject with the beat sheet line item, not with "make it better".
6. **Record the cost per delivered video.** It feeds the CAC math in
   [[05 Ad Strategy And Budget]].

## Done when
- `Production Platforms.md` and `AI Production Platforms.md` each hold the comparison and a
  named pick with the reason.
- The AI hook test ran (or was consciously skipped, with the reason written down) and its
  credit cost is recorded.
- `Shoot Order.md` exists and the first order (WP_P1_UGC_coached) has been placed with it.
- The delivered video passed the QA list and is registered in the `app/` asset browser.
- Usage rights and raw footage are in hand; cost per video recorded.

## Output
- `Production Platforms.md` — human creators: the comparison, the pick, market rates
- `AI Production Platforms.md` — the synthetic lane: the pick, the host-only rule, QA, cost
- `Production Guardrails.md` — the one-pager for both lanes: Meta dating policy, FTC bona
  fide use, disclosure, rights — pasted into every order and every AI run
- `Shoot Order.md` — the reusable order package: the five UGC scripts, specs, rights, QA
- `Host Scripts.md` — the synthetic lane's run package: five third-person host scripts
  (one per hook), the card set to capture from the app, run parameters, post-production
- `Host Run 2026-09-07.md` — the first synthetic run (S2): measured credits, where the
  workflow was overridden and why, the post-production recipe with its numbers, QA, and what
  keeps the file an internal animatic rather than an ad
- `Host Run 2026-09-08.md` — the second run (S6, the owner's sixth hook): the 480p test
  path, the first moving inset (the app screen recording), captions black-on-white
- `Host Run 2026-09-08 S7.md` — the third run (S7, campaign 7): generated straight through
  `generate_video` with no workflow and no cards, the credit split, and the finding that this
  ffmpeg has no `drawtext`/libass — captions go through PIL instead
- `Creator Brief EN+PL.pdf` (+ `EN`, `PL`) — the creator-facing export of `Shoot Order.md`;
  Polish exists only in the PDF
- Delivered creative → registered in `app/` (assets live there, not in the vault)

## Notes
The trap: briefing a creator like an actor kills the format — UGC works because it sounds
like a person, not a script. Give the beat sheet and the hook verbatim, then let the
creator phrase the middle beats in their own words; lock only the first 1.5 s and the
guardrails. Second trap: usage rights not in writing before payment. Third trap: letting an
AI presenter tell a first-person story — that is a fabricated testimonial under §11.1 and
the FTC rule, and the Higgsfield workflow's own truth gate refuses it; AI clips carry an
on-frame "AI-generated presenter" line and speak about the product, not about themselves.
