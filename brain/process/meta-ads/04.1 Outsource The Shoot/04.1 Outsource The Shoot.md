# 04.1 · Outsource The Shoot

Checklist: [[04.1 TODO]] · Canvas: [[Meta Ads.canvas|Meta Ads]]

## Goal
Every campaign brief from [[03 Choose Videos And Five Campaigns]] turned into a delivered,
spec-compliant creative by an external creator — without us holding a camera. The industry
name for this is **UGC creator sourcing** (also "creative-as-a-service" for the agency
variant) — that's the term to search and to put in job posts.

## Process
1. **Split what gets outsourced from what stays in-house.** Round one: the two statics and
   the pose→result piece are design-only (in-house), the screen capture comes from our own
   app — only `WP_P1_UGC_coached` needs an external human. One video, one order.
2. **Pick the platform once, in writing.** The comparison and the pick live in
   `Production Platforms.md`. Criteria that matter for us, in order: can we choose the
   *specific* creator (male 25–40, US English) · full **paid-ads usage rights included** ·
   accepts a dating-app brief · price per video · turnaround.
3. **Order with a package, not a chat.** The reusable order template is `Shoot Order.md`:
   the brief link, the beat sheet verbatim, the hook word-for-word, specs (9:16 master,
   sound-off legibility), the guardrail one-pager (§11 — men only, no outcome claims, no
   viewer-attribute lines), and links to reference ads (links, never files — their
   creatives are their IP).
4. **Rights before payment.** Full paid usage rights for Meta, in writing, plus the raw
   footage. A video without written ad rights cannot run — this is the most common gotcha
   on UGC marketplaces.
5. **QA against the brief, not against taste.** Accept when: every beat present, hook
   legible with sound off in 1.5 s, specs pass, §11 clean, §11.2 disclosure on the reveal.
   Reject with the beat sheet line item, not with "make it better".
6. **Record the cost per delivered video.** It feeds the CAC math in
   [[05 Ad Strategy And Budget]].

## Done when
- `Production Platforms.md` holds the comparison and a named pick with the reason.
- `Shoot Order.md` exists and the first order (WP_P1_UGC_coached) has been placed with it.
- The delivered video passed the QA list and is registered in the `app/` asset browser.
- Usage rights and raw footage are in hand; cost per video recorded.

## Output
- `Production Platforms.md` — the comparison, the pick, market rates
- `Shoot Order.md` — the reusable order package
- Delivered creative → registered in `app/` (assets live there, not in the vault)

## Notes
The trap: briefing a creator like an actor kills the format — UGC works because it sounds
like a person, not a script. Give the beat sheet and the hook verbatim, then let the
creator phrase the middle beats in their own words; lock only the first 1.5 s and the
guardrails. Second trap: usage rights not in writing before payment.
