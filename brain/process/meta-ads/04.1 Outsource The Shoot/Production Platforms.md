# Production Platforms — where to order the shoot

**The pick: Billo ($500 starter package → 3–5 videos, ~$99–150/video).** Fallback:
Backstage casting call (~$25 post + $250–400 to the actor, own contract). Researched
2026-09-02; sources inline. The workflow's industry name: **UGC sourcing** — the ad style
is "performance creative", the agency variant "creative-as-a-service".

## Why Billo wins for our job

1. **Usage rights solved structurally** — their 2026 brand ToS assigns rights irrevocably,
   worldwide, unlimited duration, **explicitly including paid media**, in the base price
   ([ToS](https://billo.app/terms-of-service-2026/)). On every other marketplace this is
   the gotcha you negotiate per order.
2. **You pick the person** — creators apply to the brief with video pitches + past work;
   demographic filters and direct invites ([help](https://help.billo.app/en/articles/4775361-reviewing-creator-applications)).
   We need a specific type: male 25–40, US English, natural on camera.
3. **The $500 minimum is not dead weight** — it funds the 3-creator × hook-variant first
   test that practitioner guidance calls for anyway. One creator = zero information about
   the hook-rate distribution.
4. Standard channel for mobile-app UGC; ~5-day creator turnaround.

Trade-offs to manage: revisions honored only against the written scope — the beat sheet in
`Shoot Order.md` must be airtight *before* ordering; credits expire in 12 months; Trustpilot
4.1★/519 shows some creator-payment friction on their side
([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/billo.app)).

restricts *dating services* — WinkyPie is a photo app, not a dating service, and the §11
third-person rule already covers the real risk.

## Briefing rules (feed these into `Shoot Order.md`)

1. **Hook verbatim, and buy hook variants** — give 2–3 exact opening lines, one per cut;
   lock only the first 1.5 s and the guardrails, let the creator phrase the middle beats.
2. **Always buy the raw footage** — one shoot becomes hook tests, a 15 s cutdown, b-roll
   and thumbnails at zero extra creator cost.
3. **Rights in writing before payment**: channels (paid social incl. Meta), duration
   (perpetual or 12+ months), editing permission, likeness. Skip exclusivity and
   whitelisting — we run from our own page.
4. **3–5 creators per concept, not one.**
5. **Ask for the opening 3 seconds early** and judge the expression change, not polish.
6. **We supply the app-screen inserts** — brief the creator to leave clean gaps and shoot
   handheld b-roll of them holding the phone so inserts cut in naturally.
