# Production Platforms — where to order the shoot

**The pick stands: Billo ($500 starter package → 3–5 videos, list $99/video, realistic
$150–250 all-in). The alternative is Upwork: one creator, one video, ~$150–300 all-in, 5%
fee, escrow, your own likeness clause — the cheaper entry when a single video is enough or
the Billo pool has no fit.** Backstage stays as the casting route if neither pool delivers
(~$25 post + $250–400 to the actor, own contract). **Sideshift was checked on 2026-09-02 and
parked** — it is a $299–999/month
creator-hiring subscription built for 5–15+ creators posting from their own accounts, the wrong
shape for one video. Researched 2026-09-02 (second pass same day); sources inline. The
workflow's industry name: **UGC sourcing** — the ad style is "performance creative", the
agency variant "creative-as-a-service". The synthetic lane (Higgsfield, TopView, Arcads…) is a
separate note: [[AI Production Platforms]].

## Why Billo wins for our job

1. **Usage rights solved structurally** — the brand ToS (effective 2026-06-01) lets us
   distribute deliverables "in any media, language, or territory" across "paid, owned, earned,
   or shared media", sublicense and make derivative works
   ([brand ToS §8.1](https://billo.app/terms-of-service-2025/)), and the creator ToS is the
   **only one of the five checked that contains an explicit likeness grant** — "use of your
   image, name, voice, likeness" plus a moral-rights waiver
   ([creator ToS §7.2–7.3](https://billo.app/terms-of-service-creators/)). AI-training use is
   excluded unless granted ([help](https://help.billo.app/en/articles/10368596-how-does-billo-work)).
   On every other marketplace this is the gotcha you negotiate per order.
2. **You pick the person** — the brief sets the creator's **age and gender**, duration
   (15/30/60/90/120 s) and 9:16; creators apply with pitches and past work; invite-only briefs
   possible ([brief fields](https://help.billo.app/en/articles/4825120-how-to-create-a-brief),
   [reviewing applicants](https://help.billo.app/en/articles/4775361-reviewing-creator-applications)).
3. **Apps are explicitly allowed** — "Apps/Services/Digital" is on the permitted list, and a
   digital-product brief runs without shipping anything
   ([allowed products](https://help.billo.app/en/articles/6211137-can-i-order-a-video-on-billo-for-my-product),
   [product types](https://help.billo.app/en/articles/6171733-how-to-create-a-product)).
4. **The $500 minimum is not dead weight** — it funds the 3-creator × hook-variant first
   test that practitioner guidance calls for anyway. One creator = zero information about
   the hook-rate distribution.
5. Standard channel for mobile-app UGC. Turnaround **7–12 days** after the creator gets the
   brief, per their own help centre ([help](https://help.billo.app/en/articles/4775471-how-long-does-it-take-to-get-a-video)) —
   not the ~5 days quoted in the first draft of this note.

**Re-verified 2026-09-02:** the public pricing page now redirects to the login screen — the
list price is no longer on the site, and the homepage sells "5,000+ creators" and "22,000+
brands" instead ([billo.app](https://billo.app/)). Third-party pricing teardowns from
2026-08 still put the list price at **$99 per video** and the realistic spend at **$150–250**
once you pay for creator customisation, with revisions outside scope adding up to ~20%
([UGC Roster, 2026-08-28](https://www.ugcroster.com/blog/brands/billo-pricing-breakdown-2026-costs-plans-hidden-fees) —
a competitor's blog, read accordingly). **Confirm the minimum package and the per-video price
at sign-up before the first order and correct this line.**

Trade-offs to manage — all of them bite hardest on a one-video order:
- **Auto-approval, no rejection.** A delivery you do not act on is approved automatically
  (help article says 3 business days `[unverified — page now 404]`); you get **two edit
  requests per video**, against the written scope only, and "all charges are final and
  non-refundable" ([help](https://help.billo.app/en/articles/10368596-how-does-billo-work),
  [brand ToS §6.9](https://billo.app/terms-of-service-2025/)). Brand reviews on G2 (2026-08) say
  exactly this: "you can't flat out reject a creative… I ended up paying for a lot of content
  we did not end up using" ([G2](https://www.g2.com/products/billo/reviews)). So: review the
  day it lands, and the beat sheet in `Shoot Order.md` must be airtight *before* ordering.
- **Raw footage is not in the base price** — creators sell it through a "got more content?"
  add-on (brand ToS §4.7 "Additional Assets"; [Capterra creator review](https://www.capterra.com/p/233597/Billo/reviews/)).
  Budget for it; the briefing rules below say why.
- **Creator pay is low** — creators report $40–90 per video after Billo's cut
  ([G2 creator review 2026-05](https://www.g2.com/products/billo/reviews), [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/billo.app?stars=1)),
  which predicts the "rushed brief" complaints. Pricing tiers seen by third parties: $99
  standard / $149 premium / $249 influencer-tier, packs $500–5,000 ([DesignRevision 2026-05](https://designrevision.com/alternatives/billo),
  [ppc.io 2026-01](https://ppc.io/blog/ugc-pricing)) — pay the premium tier for this brief.
- Credits expire in 12 months (brand ToS §5.2). Scores: Trustpilot 4.1/519, G2 4.3/39,
  Capterra 4.0/13 — the Trustpilot score is mostly creator-side five-stars, so read the 1–2★
  pages for the brand view ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/billo.app)).

## The round-one order (decided 2026-09-02)

**Order the same UGC brief from four different men, plus one human host read of the DEMO
script — not one man recording five scripts.** Four of the five hypotheses need no person on
camera (two statics, the screen demo, the pose→result pair — see the table in
[[Budget And Thresholds]]). The one that does, `WP_P1_UGC_coached`, is tested on *delivery* —
same hook, different men — because the hook is locked and the only variable left is who says
it and how; Meta's Advantage+ app campaign then routes budget to the take that works. One man
gives one data point. Each delivery: 20–30 s base + 2 alternative openings + raw footage
(a paid add-on on Billo) + the clean gap for our screen inserts, per [[Production Guardrails]].

| Scenario | What arrives | Cost | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A · Billo standard package, 5 videos** | 4 × `WP_P1_UGC_coached` from four men + 1 × human host read of `WP_P2_DEMO_freecheck` ("Would your photo pass?", phone in hand over our capture) | $500 package + raw-footage add-ons ≈ $50–150 → **≈ $550–650** | **Recommended.** Four takes on the one human hypothesis, and a human control for the AI host on the DEMO |
| B · Billo premium creators, 3 videos | 3 × `WP_P1_UGC_coached` from three men | 3 × $149 ≈ $447 + raw | If the standard pool has no fit at 25–40 US male |
| C · Upwork, one creator | 1 × `WP_P1_UGC_coached` + 2 openings + raw | $150–300 all-in | Cheapest start; **no variance test** — you learn whether *he* works, not whether the hook does |
| D · One creator records all five | one man, five scripts | ≈ $400–700 direct | **No.** Four of the scripts have no role for a person; a man reading a static's line is a worse static; and one face across five ads makes every result about him |

Round two, if `UGC_coached` wins: the winning man again with the next hook, plus two new
men on the winning hook — a person variable and a hook variable, in separate ads.

## Sideshift — what it is, and why not now

Sideshift (sideshift.app) is a **creator-hiring subscription**, not a per-video marketplace.
Brands post a job listing, creators from a claimed 900k–1M US pool apply, the platform
handles contracts, 1099s and payouts; "videos go out from the creator's own account, so they
read as real content, not an ad you placed"
([creator marketplace page](https://sideshift.app/platform/creator-marketplace)). Creator pay
is "hybrid retainer + bonuses or CPM-based"; the brand then re-runs converting videos as paid
ads on its own pixel — rights for that are bundled from the Growth tier up
([Sideshift vs Trend, 2025-12](https://sideshift.app/blog/sideshift-vs-trend)). Their own
pricing page is JavaScript-rendered and unreadable to a fetch; the marketplace page and the
blog say **$299 / $499 / $999 per month**, a third-party review from 2026-07 says
$199 / $299 / $999 plus deposit and withdrawal fees — the homepage adds a 3% processing fee
on payouts. Treat the tier prices as `[unverified]` until read in the app.

Why it does not fit round one: the platform fee alone exceeds the price of the video; the
model assumes 5–15 creators seeding organic content; the independent review reports "minimal
fraud screening", uneven quality and creators who ghost after being hired
([Launchpoint](https://www.launchpointhq.com/blog/sideshift-reviews-pricing-alternatives)).
**Revisit when [[09 Analyze KPIs]] has a proven UGC winner and the plan is 10+ creator videos
a month** — that is the job Sideshift, Insense and soona are built for.

## Briefing rules (feed these into `Shoot Order.md`)

1. **Hook verbatim, and buy hook variants** — give 2–3 exact opening lines, one per cut;
   lock only the first 1.5 s and the guardrails, let the creator phrase the middle beats.
   If the AI hook test in [[AI Production Platforms]] ran first, brief the winning hook only.
2. **Always buy the raw footage** — one shoot becomes hook tests, a 15 s cutdown, b-roll
   and thumbnails at zero extra creator cost.
3. **Rights in writing before payment**: channels (paid social incl. Meta), duration
   (perpetual or 12+ months), editing permission, likeness. Skip exclusivity and
   whitelisting — we run from our own page. **Add a derivative-edit and AI-modification
   clause** if the footage may later go through Higgsfield's `ad-multiplier` (face
   replacement) — a standard release does not obviously cover it.
4. **3–5 creators per concept, not one.**
5. **Ask for the opening 3 seconds early** and judge the expression change, not polish.
6. **We supply the app-screen inserts** — brief the creator to leave clean gaps and shoot
   handheld b-roll of them holding the phone so inserts cut in naturally.
7. **Script is a portrayal, not a testimonial.** The creator plays a demo user; no outcome
   claims, no "I got more matches", no numbers — the brief already says so. Say it in the
   order too, so the creator does not improvise proof.

Sources for the tips: [RocketShip HQ](https://www.rocketshiphq.com/find-brief-ugc-creators-mobile-app-ads/),
[bir.ch testing framework](https://bir.ch/blog/meta-ad-creative-testing-framework),
[dansugc Fiverr buyer guide](https://dansugc.com/blog/fiverr-ugc-buyer-read-2026).
Caveat: reddit.com blocked direct reading — practitioner sentiment above comes via
secondary sources.

### The post (model for `Shoot Order.md` and any direct listing)

Fields creators expect in 2026: product context, deliverables as separate line items
("1 base video + 2 alt hooks + all raw footage"), 9:16 1080p MP4/MOV H.264, "show the actual
phone", face required yes/no, hook approval before filming, 5–7 business days to first cut,
one revision round, rate visible, usage rights and term stated, payment schedule, test account
supplied ([Insense brief template](https://insense.pro/blog/ugc-brief),
[SEM Nexus app-ad brief](https://semnexus.com/how-to-build-creator-brief-that-gets-useful-ugc-for-app-ads),
[Billo, digital-product UGC](https://billo.app/blog/digital-product-ugc/)). Posts that lose
good applicants: "DM for rate", free-mail sender, "buy the subscription, we reimburse",
ID/bank before a signed deal, commission-only, vague or buried perpetual rights
([Modliflex](https://www.modliflex.com/blog/ugc-creator-scams)). Clean model to copy:
[Project Casting — Smart Marketer](https://projectcasting.com/job/ugc-casting-call-for-corporate-brand-creators)
($400/project, self-filmed, US, brand-marketing use only, no posting required).

### Vetting before money moves

1. Portfolio of 5–7 videos, not a reel; watch the first 3 s of several; consistent audio and
   framing ([Hustler Marketing](https://www.hustlermarketing.com/how-to-vet-hire-ugc-creators-what-to-look-for-before-you-commit/),
   [Conbersa](https://www.conbersa.ai/learn/how-to-vet-ugc-creators)).
2. **Intro clip shot today on their phone**: name, city, one sentence — verifies identity, US
   accent, camera comfort and the phone's picture ([Conbersa](https://www.conbersa.ai/learn/how-to-source-ugc-creators-overseas), snippet).
   Have them hold a phone with a readable screen in the same clip.
3. **Bona fide use.** If the script represents that he uses the app, he must have — FTC
   Endorsement Guides 16 CFR 255.1(c) ([text](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/255.1)),
   and people presented as actual consumers who are not need a clear disclosure (255.2(c),
   [text](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/255.2)). Our confession hook represents use.
   Cheapest fix: he runs the free check and one render on his own selfie before the shoot, and
   warrants it in the contract. This is not yet in the brief — add it to `Shoot Order.md`.
4. 50% on signature, 50% within 48 h of accepted delivery; PayPal Goods & Services (2.99% to
   the receiver, buyer can dispute) or Wise; never Friends & Family, Venmo, crypto
   ([UGC Roster](https://www.ugcroster.com/blog/best-payment-methods-for-ugc-creators),
   [PayPal fees](https://www.paypal.com/us/webapps/mpp/merchant-fees)). Whether PayPal's
   protection covers files delivered as a service: `[unverified]`.
