## The toolchain finding — this ffmpeg cannot burn text

**The Homebrew ffmpeg on this Mac has no `drawtext`, no `subtitles` and no `ass` filter**
(486 filters, built without libfreetype and libass). The caption recipe in
[[Production Guardrails]] rule 5 is written for ASS BorderStyle 3 and **will not run here.**

The way round it, used for this build and reusable:

1. Render every caption as a transparent PNG in Python/PIL — black text on a white rounded
   box — and lay it on with `overlay ... enable='between(t,a,b)'`. Same look, no libass.
2. Rounded corners on the host inset: a white-on-black rounded-rect PNG scaled to the inset
   size, then `alphamerge` into the video before `overlay`.
3. A disclosure or watermark that must survive both bright and dark frames gets its own soft
   shadow baked into the PNG (PIL `GaussianBlur` under the glyphs) rather than a scrim.
4. `zoompan` must be fed **one frame**, not a `-loop 1 -t` still, or it multiplies frames and
   a 23 s wall renders as 571 s.
5. Caption timings come from `whisper-cli -ml 1` (word level), grouped into whole phrases by
   matching each phrase's letters against the word stream. Splitting on line length instead
   breaks captions mid-clause.

## Notes for the next run

- **The wall images arrived at 768×1024** — downscaled somewhere before upload, so the
  1080×1920 frame upscales ~1.9× and crops 25 % of the width. Get the originals before spend.
- **One shirt in all twelve frames.** The script turned it into the proof line ("same shirt
  you had on") rather than hiding it; a second outfit would widen the format.
- **The middle montage runs ~1.0 s per frame** because twelve results have to fit the voice
  track. That is faster than the swipe's 1.8 s and it is where a licensed music bed would
  earn its keep. No music is licensed, so none was added.
- **Seedance drifts on unlocked lines.** Take one said "same shirt **you** had on" where the
  script said "he" — kept, because this note already allows second person about the viewer's
  photo. Locked lines came back verbatim on every take.
- The model recommends a preset ("IN THE DARK") for this kind of prompt; decline it with
  `declined_preset_id` or the second clip stops matching the first.
