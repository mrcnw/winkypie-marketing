# Brief — WP_P2_STATIC_100ms

**Single variable tested:** the decision-moment angle ("her swipe is decided by the first
photo, in an instant") against the other four angles. Everything else — persona P2, static
format, CTA — held constant with campaign 5.

**Evidence:** the niche's longest-living creatives are low-fi statics (Roast 104–131 days);
Photoshoot Dating's 43-day veteran dramatizes exactly this moment. See
[[Winning Ads 2026-09-02]]. Re-read 2026-09-06: Roast's "Same face" image family is the most-cloned
thing on its page (~257 ads); the top one (2014738899400393) ran 2026-04-24 → 2026-09-02 and
the family keeps running with new variants ([[ROAST]]). Low-fi static is still the proven look.

## Hook (verbatim, must be legible in 1.5 s, sound off)

> She decided in **100 ms**.

Locked line (PRODUCT.md §1). Narrative device — never presented as a statistic, no source
citation, no percentage anywhere on the creative.

## Layout (single frame)

- Dark `#0E0E0E` ground, warm amber-pink radial glow. "100 ms" is the italic-gradient punch
  word (the one brand gradient).
- Beat two, smaller — **the blame-shift, on the creative itself** (every proven ad in the
  niche states the problem on-frame, not only in primary text): "First photo. First
  chance." — the locked problem line (PRODUCT.md §1), the same words as onboarding
  screen 2, so the ad and the app say one thing. Replaced "The first photo does all the
  work." on 2026-09-06: every winner in the niche puts the dating context on frame.
- Beat three: "Be the right swipe." — the locked closing CTA.
- Footer: App Store badge + "3 days free. Cancel anytime."
- Optional motion version: the beats fade in sequence, 6 s loop. No other animation.
