# Brief — WP_P1_UGC_coached

**Single variable tested:** the coaching angle — "you don't need the skill, it coaches you"
— in the format the niche's winners use for it (UGC talking head).

**Evidence:** pose coaching is verified unique to us ([[Competitor Landscape]] scoreboard);
ReGen's own copy sells the opposite ("no guesswork — the system handles it"), so the
contrast is clean. UGC-confession is the dominant proven format on the Roast pages.
Re-read 2026-09-06: Roast's newest wave (2026-08-26 → 08-31) is seven UGC variants of
one concept, 0:37–0:50, selling the AI photos themselves with "No photographer needed" —
the leader's front door is moving to our core promise ([[ROAST]]). Their story ends on a
deception ("she still thinks he owns a yacht"); ours ends on the photo.

## Hook (spoken + overlay, 0.0–1.5 s)

> My Hinge profile was four group shots and a gym mirror selfie.

Revised 2026-09-06 from "All my photos were group shots and gym mirror selfies.": the
winners name the app and the profile in the first line ("his hinge was dead", "joined
Hinge the same week"); ours does the same about *his photos*, never about his results.
The app name and the photo count are the creator's own — he says the app he actually uses
and what his photos actually were; the shape is locked (bona fide use, [[Production Guardrails]]).
Customer language, from the step-02 doc's own hook example. Confession-story shape
— the proven UGC formula in this niche is problem → discovery → twist → result, not a
feature tour. The coaching is the **twist**, not the opener; "You don't need to know how to
pose." moves to the overlay at the twist beat.

## Production flags (from step 04.1, 2026-09-02 — resolve before the order goes out)

Full handling in [[Production Guardrails]]; sources in [[Production Platforms]].

1. **He must have used the app.** The hook ("all my photos were…", "tried the AI photo
   thing") represents use; FTC 16 CFR 255.1(c) requires a bona fide user, so "actor plays a
   demo user" is not enough on its own. Deliverable zero: his own result screenshot before the
   shoot; warranty in the contract. The hook stays — it is honest once he has used it.
2. **The 9 s beat is shot twice.** Meta's dating policy bans "selfies, images where a person
   is visibly photographing themselves" in dating ads, and whether WinkyPie counts as "dating
   facilitation" is unresolved ([[06 Meta Ads Configuration]]). Variant A as written; variant B
   with the selfie implied by the cut, phone in hand only. Master chosen when Meta answers.
3. **On-frame text is added by us**, not the creator — overlays, §11.2, end card.
4. **Human only.** This confession-story format is not produced on the AI lane; a generated
   presenter may only deliver host copy — see [[AI Production Platforms]].
