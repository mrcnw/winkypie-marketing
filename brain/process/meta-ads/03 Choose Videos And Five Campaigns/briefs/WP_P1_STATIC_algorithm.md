# Brief — WP_P1_STATIC_algorithm

**Single variable tested:** the blame-shift angle — it is not the app and not the algorithm,
it is the first photo — against the decision-moment static (campaign 1). Same static format,
same second and third beats, same CTA; only the hook differs, so the read is the angle and
nothing else.

**Evidence:** the blame-shift is the most consistent reframe on the Roast page and the common
mechanism of every winner in [[Winning Ads 2026-09-02]] ("it's not your face / not the
algorithm / not Tinder; the thing between you and matches is fixable and cheap"). Live on
2026-09-06: "Most guys blame Tinder. The problem is the photos." (2213072699509422, 123 days),
"Stop blaming the algorithm…" (1524577909408074, a 0:12 silent video, 87 days — one of the
longest-running videos on the page), "It was never the face." (2088720791726438), Photoshoot
Dating's "It's the photos, not you." ([[ROAST]], [[Roast AI Creative Teardown 2026-09-06]]).
No campaign of ours tested it as the hook; campaign 1 carries it only as beat two.

Replaced `WP_P1_STATIC_proprices` on 2026-09-06: across ~980 ads read on the Roast AI and
Confessions of a Dater pages not one runs on price, and the savings brief had called itself
the kill-first candidate. The price hook is parked in [[Hooks And Angles]] (Backup).

## Hook (verbatim, must be legible in 1.5 s, sound off)

> Not the algorithm. **The first photo.**

Third person about the app and the photo — nothing about the man. Roast's versions ("Stop
blaming the algorithm. The reason you're getting no matches…", "signs you're shadowbanned")
diagnose the viewer's results; ours does not. No "you're not ugly", no "no matches", no
percentages, no "shadowban".

## Layout (single frame — campaign 1's layout, hook swapped)

- Dark `#0E0E0E` ground, warm amber-pink radial glow (PRODUCT.md §10). "The first photo" is
  the italic-gradient punch (the one brand gradient).
- Beat two, smaller: "First photo. First chance." — the locked problem line.
- Beat three: "Be the right swipe." — the locked closing CTA.
- Footer: App Store badge.
- No people, no generated imagery → no §11.2 needed. If a result image is added later, the
  disclosure comes with it.
- Optional motion version: the beats fade in sequence, 6 s loop — the same build as campaign 1.

## Primary text

> It was never the algorithm. On Hinge, Tinder and Bumble the first photo does the work.
> WinkyPie turns one selfie into that photo — and checks your selfie for free before
> anything costs you.

Support line (backup hook, same idea): "Not the app. Not luck. The first photo."
