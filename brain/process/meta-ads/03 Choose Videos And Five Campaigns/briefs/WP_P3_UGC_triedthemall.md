# Brief — WP_P3_UGC_triedthemall

**Single variable tested:** the switcher's likeness angle — a man who has tried other AI
photo apps and stopped because the results were not him — against the coaching confession
(campaign 3). Same UGC format, same casting, same closer; only the story differs, so the
read is the angle.

**Evidence:** the owner's idea, added 2026-09-07 as the sixth campaign ("I've tested a lot
of AI photo apps and nothing works — it wasn't me, my body, not realistic and authentic.
And finally I found the best app for that."). It sits on the persona's first two buying
blockers in PRODUCT.md §3 — *thinks AI photos look fake* · *unsure it will still look like
him* — and on the competitor gap that matters: ROAST's most common bad review is "it didn't
look like me" ([[ROAST]], [[Competitor Landscape]]). No campaign in the round-one set puts
the likeness promise in a human mouth; `WP_P3_POSERESULT_stillyou` says it as a static.
Set size: step 03 fixes five campaigns; this sixth is the owner's call and lifts the ad set
to seven creatives — above Meta's "up to six creative options" guidance in
[[Budget And Thresholds]], so one of the six waits for wave two or this one does.

## Hook (spoken + overlay, 0.0–1.5 s)

> I tried every AI photo app. None of them looked like me.

The owner's line, cut to the voice: two sentences, hard stops, the problem named in the
first breath. "Every" is his honest experience or it is not said — see the production flags;
the fallback line is "I didn't trust AI photo apps. None of them looked like the guy in the
mirror." No app is named, ever. "The best app" from the seed idea is not spoken: a
superlative is a claim, and the proof is the result on his phone, not his verdict.

| 25–33 | End card | "Authentic pro photos. More matches." · "Be the right swipe." + App Store badge |

He talks about his photos and the apps, never his dating life and never his results. The
other apps are "the ones I tried" — no names, no logos, no screenshots of anyone else's
product. The story ends at the photo.

## Primary text

> Tried the AI photo apps and got someone else's face? WinkyPie starts from a pose, coaches
> you, and renders your selfie in the scene. Your face, your build. Looks pro. Still you.

## Production flags (resolve before the order goes out)

Full handling in [[Production Guardrails]], Lane A.

1. **Two things he must have actually done:** used WinkyPie on his own selfie (deliverable
   zero, warranty in the contract) *and* tried other AI photo apps, if he says "every". The
   FTC Endorsement Guides make the tried-them-all line his experience; if he has not, the
   fallback hook above is the one he says.
2. **The 9 s beat is shot twice** — selfie on frame, and phone-in-hand only — until Meta's
   dating classification is answered ([[06 Meta Ads Configuration]]).
3. **On-frame text is ours**: overlays, §11.2, the end card. He burns nothing in.
4. **Human only.** First-person experience is never generated on the AI lane
   ([[AI Production Platforms]]).
