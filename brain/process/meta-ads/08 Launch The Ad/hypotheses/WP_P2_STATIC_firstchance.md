# Hypothesis — WP_P2_STATIC_firstchance

**Draft. Not yet committed — `dated:` is empty and stays empty until the moment before spend
starts.** A hypothesis written after the numbers arrive always turns out to have predicted
them ([[08 Launch The Ad]]), which is the whole reason this file exists before the campaign
does.

## The line

> If we show **"First photo. First chance."** as two photographs to **P2 Grinder**, then it
> lands in the **top two of round one on link CTR**, because the argument is carried by the
> photographs instead of asserted in type — the one thing no other static in the set does.

## Why it is relative, not a number

[[KPI]] is explicit: **round one has no baseline.** The absolute figures in its targets table
are all marked `[unsourced]` and exist to orient, not to decide. So the prediction is a
ranking inside the set, which is a claim the first round can actually settle. The first
measured CPI becomes the baseline everything after this is judged against.

## What would falsify it

Bottom two on link CTR at the impression floor. That would say the photographic carrier is
not what moves this angle, and campaign 1's type card is the better vehicle for it — which is
exactly the question this creative was cut to answer.
