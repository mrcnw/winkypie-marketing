# Hypothesis — WP_P2_STATIC_swipingback

**Written at launch, 2026-09-26, minutes after the switches went on and before any number
existed.** The prediction is the brief's variable, applied to the cut the owner chose (v3: the
v0 picture with his voice-over, black lead-in trimmed). Not edited again.

## The line

> If we show **the profile as a set** — four cards of the same man, distance ticking down,
> a voice-over saying what the cards show — to **P2 Grinder**, then it lands in the **top two
> of the smoke test on link CTR**, because a man does not buy a photo, he buys a profile, and
> this is the only creative in the set that shows the profile.

## Read delivery first

The first plate, "Is nobody swiping back?", asks the viewer about his own results — the shape
PRODUCT.md §11 gives as the worked example of what Meta rejects. So the first thing this ad
measures is whether it **serves at all**. A rejection is a result about that plate, not about
the profile-as-a-set angle, and it settles nothing about the variable. Keep the stages apart.

## What would falsify it

Served, and bottom of the three on link CTR at the impression floor in [[KPI]]. That would say
the set does not out-argue a single strong frame, and the next cut goes back to one photograph.

## What it cannot settle

It is a smoke test at $15 a day: three ads, no learning-phase exit, CTR at a few thousand
impressions each. The reading is directional. It says nothing about CPI, and the two drifted
brand lines on the plates (recorded in the v2 and v3 sidecars) ride along untested.
