## What makes this one different from the others

purpose**. "Why you're not getting matches." asserts the viewer's dating results, which is
Meta's personal-attributes rule and the most common rejection cause in this category. So the
first thing this campaign measures is not CTR — it is **whether it is approved at all**.

That makes the read two-stage, and the stages must not be confused:

1. **Delivery.** Approved and serving, or rejected. A rejection is a result about the
   headline, not about the format, and it says nothing about the variable this campaign was
   cut to test.
2. **CTR against campaign 2**, and only among impressions that actually served.
