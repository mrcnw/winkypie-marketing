# WP_IG_EDU1_firstphoto — The first photo does all the work

The first Education carousel for `@winkypie.app`. Built to the spec in
[[Static Post Format]], slotted in [[Posts Calendar]] on 2026-09-16, topic #1 of
[[Post Ideas]]. Ten 4:5 text cards, no photography except slide 9.

**The idea:** teach the seven rules a first photo has to pass, then show that the app is what
makes passing them possible without a photographer. Nothing here promises a result; the three
numbers on the slides are industry averages and each carries its `PRODUCT.md` §8 label on the
frame. The full source list is the first comment.

## The hook — slide 1, and the grid tile

```
The first photo
does all the work.

7 rules · swipe
```

*all the work* is the punch word: Fraunces italic, brand gradient. `7 rules · swipe` sits at
the bottom in Inter 34 px, `#8A8A8A`. This is the tile judged at 160 px in the grid, so
nothing else goes on it.

It is the promoted backup hook in [[Hooks And Angles]] — the same line campaign 1 carries on
frame, which is the point: organic repeats what paid says, in the same words.

## The carousel — every slide, verbatim

Three beats per slide: **the claim** → **the detail** → **the fix** in `#8A8A8A`.

| # | Beat 1 — the claim | Beat 2 — the detail | Beat 3 — the fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | **Face the lens.** | Forward-facing photos are 102 % more likely to get a like. `Hinge · 2023` | Body angled, face forward. The turn-away shot is photo four, not photo one. |
| 3 | **One face in the frame.** | A group shot in slot one asks her to work out which one is you. She won't. | Solo first. Friends earn a later slot. |
| 4 | **Nothing across the eyes.** | Sunglasses and a low cap remove the first thing she reads. | Eyes visible in the first photo. Save the sunglasses for the beach one. |
| 5 | **Fill the frame.** | Posts with a face get 38 % more engagement. `Image-engagement · 2025` | Chest up. A wide landscape with you thirty metres away is a landscape. |
| 6 | **One full-body in the set.** | Profiles with a full-body photo get 203 % more messages. `Match · 2024` | Not first. Second or third, standing, whole body in frame. |
| 7 | **Real place, real light.** | A white wall reads as a work photo. A street, a café, a terrace gives her a first line. | Outdoors, the hour before sunset. |
| 8 | **Six photos, six places.** | One outfit and one wall six times looks like one afternoon, because it was. | Change the scene, the distance and the mood across the set. |
