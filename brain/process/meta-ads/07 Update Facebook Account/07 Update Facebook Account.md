# 07 · Update Facebook Account

Checklist: [[07 TODO]] · Drawing: [[Process.excalidraw|Process]]

## Goal
The Facebook page and Business Manager in a state that will not stop a launch: verified,
branded, permissioned, with billing that works and no policy flags outstanding.

## Process
1. **Do this early.** Business verification and payment-method review take days, not
   minutes, and both can block a launch that was otherwise ready. This step's real cost is
   waiting, so start the clock before you need it.
2. **Ownership before access.** Assets owned by a personal profile rather than Business
   Manager are a permanent risk — one lost account and the ad history goes with it. Move
   them, then grant access, never the reverse.
3. **The Page is a landing surface.** People do click the profile from an ad. An empty Page
   with a 2024 cover image undercuts everything the ad just claimed. A handful of real posts
   is enough; it does not need a content strategy.
4. **Check Account Quality first, not after a rejection.** Existing restrictions silently
   throttle delivery, and you will misread the result as a bad creative.
5. **Branding matches the app, and the app is canonical.** Copy flows app → web → ads,
   never the reverse — see the root `CLAUDE.md`.
6. **Two humans with admin access.** One admin is an outage waiting to happen.

## Done when
- Business verification is complete or explicitly not required.
- A working payment method is on file and the currency is correct.
- Account Quality shows no active restrictions.
- Page branding matches the app, and the Instagram account is linked.
- At least two admins, both with 2FA on.

## Output
- `Account Setup.md` — what is configured, who has access, what is still pending

## Notes
Access lists rot fast. Put a date next to each name in the doc and re-check it at the start
of every campaign round.
